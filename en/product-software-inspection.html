Inspection System

Planning · Execution · Reporting · Traceability

The Inspection System digitises patrol and inspection workflows, ensuring equipment and facility safety.

Key Features

Inspection Planning
Customisable inspection routes, checkpoints, standards and frequencies.
Mobile Execution
NFC / QR code / GPS checkpoint confirmation, with photo and video evidence.
Exception Handling
Automatic alerts for abnormal findings, with work order generation and closed-loop tracking.
Inspection Reports
Automatic generation of inspection reports, with data visualisation and trend analysis.
Equipment Management
Full-lifecycle management of equipment files, maintenance records and spare parts.
Knowledge Base
Accumulation and sharing of inspection experience, supporting intelligent fault diagnosis.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for construction site safety inspections, equipment routine inspections, property environment patrols and production line quality inspections. Linked with the work order system to achieve closed-loop management from problem discovery to abnormal reporting, work order dispatch and rectification acceptance.

System Advantages

Applicable to property management, industrial parks, data centres and infrastructure facilities. Supports offline inspection with automatic data synchronisation when the network is restored.

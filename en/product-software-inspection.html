Inspection System

Planning · Execution · Reporting · Traceability

The Inspection System digitises patrol and inspection workflows, ensuring equipment and facility safety.

Key Features

Inspection Planning

Customisable inspection routes, checkpoints, standards and frequencies.

Mobile Execution

NFC / QR code / GPS checkpoint confirmation, with photo and video evidence.

Exception Handling

Automatic alerts for abnormal findings, with work order generation and closed-loop tracking.

Inspection Reports

Automatic generation of inspection reports, with data visualisation and trend analysis.

Equipment Management

Full-lifecycle management of equipment files, maintenance records and spare parts.

Knowledge Base

Accumulation and sharing of inspection experience, supporting intelligent fault diagnosis.

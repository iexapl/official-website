Solar & Energy Storage Solution

Solar PV · Storage · Smart Dispatch · Monitoring

AXLTeco solar and energy storage solution provides clean, reliable green energy for buildings and enterprises, reducing electricity costs and improving energy independence.

System Architecture

The system consists of four subsystems: solar power generation, energy storage, energy management and load supply. The PV subsystem includes panels, inverters and combiner equipment; the storage subsystem comprises battery packs, BMS and storage inverters; the EMS optimises charge/discharge strategies and grid-tied/off-grid switching.

Solution Components

Solar Power Generation
Rooftop/ground-mounted PV panels, inverters, combiner boxes and distribution cabinets.
Energy Storage System
Lithium battery cabinets for peak shaving, backup power and output smoothing.
Smart Dispatch
Integrated solar-storage-charging energy management system optimising strategies.
Grid-tied / Off-grid
Support for grid-tied power sales and off-grid independent operation.
Monitoring & Management
Real-time monitoring of generation, storage status and load consumption with revenue analytics.
EV Charging
Supporting EV charging piles for integrated solar-storage-charging operation.

Application Architecture

The application architecture adopts cloud + local dual mode. The local EMS collects PV, storage and load data in real time and executes optimal strategies; the cloud platform provides remote monitoring and analytics; the user app supports generation viewing and alerts.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to commercial and industrial rooftops, factory parks, off-grid villages, microgrids and telecom base station backup power. In areas with unstable grid supply, the system provides reliable independent power.

Solution Advantages

High-efficiency PV panels with industry-leading conversion rates; intelligent EMS algorithms maximising self-consumption; modular storage design for flexible expansion; dual grid-tied and off-grid operation; remote monitoring reducing maintenance costs.

Solution Value

Reduce electricity costs and carbon emissions, relieve grid pressure and provide reliable backup power in unstable areas. Achieve green energy economic value through self-consumption and surplus power sales.

Project Practice

Delivered solar and energy storage solutions for multiple factories, commercial buildings and off-grid communities, covering full lifecycle from design to commissioning and O&M services.

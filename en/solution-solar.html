Solar & Energy Storage Solution

Solar · Storage · Smart Dispatch · Monitoring · Charging

AXLTeco's solar and energy storage solution provides clean, reliable green energy supply for buildings and enterprises, reducing electricity costs and improving energy independence.

Solution Components

Solar Power
Roof / ground-mounted PV modules, inverters, combiner boxes and distribution cabinets for efficient power generation.
Energy Storage
Lithium battery energy storage cabinets for peak shaving, backup power and output smoothing.
Smart Dispatch
Integrated PV-storage-charging energy management system for optimised charge/discharge strategy.
Grid-Tie / Off-Grid
Support grid-tie power sales and off-grid independent operation, flexibly adapting to different scenarios.
Monitoring
Real-time monitoring of power generation, storage status and load consumption, with revenue analysis reports.
EV Charging
Supporting electric vehicle charging piles for integrated PV-storage-charging operation.

Solution Value

Reduce electricity expenses, cut carbon emissions, alleviate grid supply pressure and provide reliable backup power in areas with unstable electricity supply.

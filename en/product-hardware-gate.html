Vehicle Barrier Gate

Fast · Stable · Intelligent · Durable

AXLTeco provides vehicle barrier gates for parking lots, communities and industrial parks, supporting licence plate recognition and remote control.

Key Features

License Plate Recognition
HD camera automatic licence plate recognition with >99% accuracy.
Bluetooth Recognition
Support mobile Bluetooth automatic recognition for frictionless access.
RFID Recognition
Support RFID tag long-range recognition, suitable for monthly-pass vehicles.
Automatic Barrier
Automatic barrier lift after recognition, high passage efficiency.
Remote Control
Support remote barrier open, close and status monitoring.

Product Series

Straight Barrier
Simple structure, fast rise/fall, suitable for standard parking lots.
Fence Barrier
Good protection effect, suitable for residential and factory entrances.
Advertising Barrier
Barrier arm can carry advertisements, suitable for commercial complexes.
AI Recognition Unit
Integrates licence plate recognition, barrier control and charging management in one unit.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for parking lots, construction site entrances, residential access control and commercial complexes. Supports linkage with parking management and charging systems for unattended intelligent access.

System Advantages

High recognition rate and fast response; vehicles pass without stopping. Deep integration with parking management systems supports multiple charging modes and report statistics. Rugged and durable equipment adapted to Africa's outdoor high-temperature and dusty environments.

Hotel/Community IPTV Solution

HD · On-Demand · Interactive · Customisable · Manageable

AXLTeco provides IPTV system solutions for hotels, serviced apartments and residential communities, offering rich television and interactive services.

Solution Components

Live TV
HD live broadcast of local and international channels, supporting time-shift and catch-up.
Video on Demand
Rich film and television resources, supporting category search and personalised recommendations.
Hotel Services
Online ordering, room service, laundry and scenic spot recommendations.
Information Display
Hotel announcements, weather, flight information and local news.
Customisable Interface
Brand-customised UI design to enhance hotel brand image.
Centralised Management
Unified content distribution, device monitoring and fault alarm via the management platform.

Solution Value

Supports integration with hotel PMS systems for automatic room information synchronisation. Supports multi-language and multi-currency display.

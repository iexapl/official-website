Hotel & Community IPTV Solution

TV · VOD · Info · Multi-room

AXLTeco provides IP-based TV entertainment and information services for hotels and residential communities.

Solution Architecture

The IPTV system adopts a four-layer microservices architecture: Headend - Transmission - Terminal - Operations. The headend layer completes TV signal reception, encoding, transcoding, and content injection. The transmission layer distributes AV streams over IP networks, with Keepalived + Nginx active-standby ensuring core streaming media high availability. The platform layer builds five microservices—Live, VOD, EPG, User, and Billing—on .NET + Furion, using RabbitMQ to orchestrate content publishing and subscription events, Redis Cluster caching program guides and hot content metadata, MySQL storing user and billing data, and MongoDB storing content tags and viewing behavior logs. The operations console is built with Vue 3 + Element Plus for content scheduling and multi-tenant management, with terminals supporting Android TV, set-top boxes, and mobile apps, enabling independent brand customization for hotels or communities.

Solution Components

Live TV
Support for hundreds of satellite, cable and online channels in HD/4K quality.
Video on Demand
Extensive content library of movies, series and shows with pay-per-view support.
Information Publishing
Custom content including welcome messages, service guides and community notices.
Multi-room Management
Independent channel lists and permissions per room, centrally managed.
Guest Services
TV-based ordering, wake-up calls, laundry and taxi booking services.
System Management
Content publishing, user management, billing and device monitoring backend.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to hotel rooms, lobbies, community lounges, serviced apartments and care homes. Provides guests with rich TV entertainment and convenient information services.

Solution Advantages

IP-based transmission eliminating coaxial cable needs; multi-screen interaction across TV, phone and tablet; flexible content customisation with brand integration; cloud or on-premise deployment.

Solution Value

Enhance guest satisfaction and hotel brand image. Generate additional revenue through value-added services. Reduce traditional cable TV infrastructure and maintenance costs.

Project Practice

Deployed IPTV systems for multiple hotels and communities, from mid-range business hotels to luxury resorts, with stable operation and positive feedback.

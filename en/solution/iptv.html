Hotel/Community IPTV Solution

Live TV · VoD · Information Publishing

For hotels, residential communities, long-term rental apartments and nursing homes, an IP network-based TV entertainment and information publishing solution that addresses the core pain points of high cabling costs, difficult content customization and few value-added services in traditional cable TV, achieving integrated IP-based TV entertainment and information services.

Solution Overview

Provides IP network-based TV entertainment and information publishing services for hotels, residential communities, long-term rental apartments and nursing homes, addressing high cabling costs, difficult content customization and few value-added services in traditional cable TV, and achieving integrated IP-based TV entertainment and information services.

Applicable to: Hotel guest rooms, hotel lobbies, community living rooms, long-term rental apartments, nursing homes.

  • IP network transmission, no separate coaxial cabling needed
  • Multi-screen interaction across TV, phone and tablet
  • Flexible content customization with brand placement support
  • Cloud or local deployment to fit different scales

Benefits: Higher guest satisfaction, additional revenue from value-added services, and lower cabling and maintenance costs.

Business Challenges

Operating hotels and residential communities, customers often face high TV cabling costs, non-customizable content and missing value-added services, and urgently need an IP-based, customizable TV and information platform that can carry value-added services.

High cabling cost

Traditional cable TV needs separate coaxial cabling — complex construction and costly maintenance.

No content customization

Fixed channel lineups leave no room for hotel branding or service guides.

Missing value-added services

The TV cannot reach room service, wake-up calls or bill queries.

Per-room management drags operations

TVs configured one by one are hard to manage centrally; guest experience and operating efficiency both suffer.

IP multi-screen TV service

IP-network delivery should cover TV, phone and tablet.

Branded content operation

Brand placement, custom welcome pages and value-added service entries are needed.

Typical Scenarios: Hotel guest-room entertainmentCommunity announcementsApartment information servicesCare content for nursing homes

Target Roles: Hotel operations managers, community property managers, IT operations staff.

Solution Architecture

Built on a four-layer “content-transmission-platform-terminal” architecture covering content management, multi-terminal delivery and guest-service integration, with support for hotel PMS integration and cloud or local deployment.

TerminalsAndroid TVIP set-top boxesPhone/tablet multi-screen interaction
▼
PlatformContent scheduling backendMulti-tenant managementDesktop operations client
▼
TransmissionIP network distributionCDN edge delivery
▼
Content SourcesSatellite/cable/OTT signalsEncoding & transcodingLocal content serverBranded content

Satellite, cable and OTT signals are encoded into the content library; the local server hosts branded content; the IP network and CDN distribute streams to every terminal; the platform layer schedules content and manages tenants.

Core Modules

Multi-source content

Satellite, cable and OTT signals unified with flexible channel scheduling.

Branded content

Hotel brand placement, personalized welcome pages and service guides.

Guest service entry

Room ordering, wake-up calls, laundry, taxi and bill queries on the TV.

Central multi-room management

Per-room configuration, batch management and status monitoring.

Cloud or local deployment

Choose the deployment model that fits the property's scale.

Integration Capabilities:

  • Integrates with hotel PMS for automatic welcome-page customization and bill queries
  • Guest services (ordering, wake-up calls, laundry, taxi) accessible from the TV
  • Per-room configuration with centralized management

Advantages & Delivery

With an IP-based TV service platform and hotel business integration capability, we support higher guest satisfaction and new value-added revenue, delivered through network assessment, platform deployment, content configuration and training.

Differentiating Advantages

No coaxial cabling

Shares the IP network infrastructure, eliminating separate cabling cost.

Branding & welcome pages

Guest-room TVs present the hotel brand and personalized greetings.

Value-added revenue entry

In-room service entries on the TV create additional income such as room ordering.

Flexible deployment

Cloud or local deployment to match different scales.

Customer Value: Higher guest satisfaction and brand image, additional revenue from value-added services, and lower cabling and maintenance costs than traditional cable TV.

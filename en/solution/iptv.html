Hotel/Community IPTV Solution

Live TV · VoD · Information Publishing

For hotels, residential communities, long-term rental apartments and nursing homes, an IP network-based TV entertainment and information publishing solution that addresses the core pain points of high cabling costs, difficult content customization and few value-added services in traditional cable TV, achieving integrated IP-based TV entertainment and information services.

Solution Overview

Provides IP network-based TV entertainment and information publishing services for hotels, residential communities, long-term rental apartments and nursing homes, addressing high cabling costs, difficult content customization and few value-added services in traditional cable TV, and achieving integrated IP-based TV entertainment and information services.

Applicable to: Hotel guest rooms, hotel lobbies, community living rooms, long-term rental apartments, nursing homes.

  • IP network transmission, no separate coaxial cabling needed
  • Multi-screen interaction across TV, phone and tablet
  • Flexible content customization with brand placement support
  • Cloud or local deployment to fit different scales

Benefits: Higher guest satisfaction, additional revenue from value-added services, and lower cabling and maintenance costs.

Business Challenges

In hotel and community operations, traditional TV services with high costs, poor experience and little value-added potential no longer meet the needs of modern guests.

  • High cabling costs: Traditional cable TV requires separate coaxial cabling, which is complex to install and costly to maintain
  • No content customization: Fixed channels cannot carry hotel branding, service guides or other personalized content
  • Missing value-added services: The TV cannot access in-room dining, wake-up calls, bill inquiries and other services
  • Scattered management: TVs in multiple rooms must be configured one by one, making centralized management difficult

Typical Scenarios: Hotel guest room entertainment, community announcement publishing, long-term rental apartment information services, caring content delivery in nursing homes.

Intended Users: Hotel operations managers, community property managers, IT operations staff.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a four-layer architecture of content sources, transmission, platform and terminals, delivering IP-based TV entertainment and information services for hotels and communities.

  1. Content layer: Satellite/cable/OTT signals are encoded and transcoded into the content library; local content servers support brand customization
  2. Transmission layer: Audio-video streams distributed over the IP network, with CDN nodes serving nearby to reduce latency
  3. Platform layer: Content scheduling backend with multi-tenant management; desktop operations client for fine-grained operations
  4. Terminal layer: Android TV, IP set-top boxes and mobile devices for multi-terminal coverage

Integration Capabilities:

  • Connected with hotel PMS systems for automatic welcome page customization and bill inquiry
  • TV entry points for guest services (in-room dining, wake-up calls, laundry, taxi)
  • Independent configuration per room with centralized management

Advantages & Delivery

With the IP-based TV service platform at its core, AXLTeco provides flexible, customizable and value-rich IPTV solutions.

Key Differentiators:

  • No separate coaxial cabling; shares infrastructure with the network system
  • Hotel brand placement and personalized welcome pages
  • Guest service entry points on TV create value-added revenue
  • Flexible choice of cloud or local deployment

Customer Value: Higher guest satisfaction and brand image, additional revenue from value-added services, and lower traditional cable TV costs.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Network assessment → Platform deployment → Content configuration → Terminal installation → Training and handover.

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, content update support, remote operations.

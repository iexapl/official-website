Smart Parking Solution

Space Guidance · Unmanned · Mobile Payment

For commercial complexes, residential compounds, transport hubs and roadside parking, a fully unmanned entry-to-exit management solution that addresses the core pain points of low parking turnover, high labor costs and difficult space hunting, achieving unattended operation, smart guidance and frictionless payment.

Solution Overview

Provides fully unmanned entry-to-exit management solutions for commercial complexes, residential compounds, transport hubs and roadside parking, addressing low turnover, high labor costs and difficult space hunting, and achieving unattended operation, smart guidance and frictionless payment.

Applicable to: Commercial complexes, residential compounds, airport/train station parking, roadside parking.

  • Raises parking turnover by more than 30%
  • Cuts labor costs by 60%
  • Average space hunting time shortened to under 2 minutes
  • Running stably for over 24 months across multiple Lagos projects

Business Challenges

In parking lot operations, peak-hour congestion, rising labor costs and poor driver experience are the three pain points constraining operating performance.

  • Low entry efficiency: Manual ticketing and registration take time, causing long queues at entrances during peak hours
  • Difficult space hunting: Drivers search blindly for spaces after entering, with disorderly vehicle flow inside the lot
  • Long payment queues: Manual exit collection is inefficient, with severe queuing at peak times
  • High management costs: Every entrance/exit needs toll staff, with high labor costs and error risk
  • Opaque data: No real-time statistics on space utilization or revenue, leaving operations decisions without evidence

Typical Scenarios: Weekend peaks at shopping centers, morning and evening peaks in residential compounds, long-term airport parking, short-term roadside parking.

Intended Users: Parking lot operators, property management staff, drivers.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a three-tier architecture of entrances/exits, in-lot systems and management platform, achieving fully unmanned entry-to-exit management.

  1. Entrance/exit layer: License plate recognition cameras and automatic barriers - automatic plate capture and gate raise on entry, automatic billing and settlement on exit
  2. In-lot layer: Ultrasonic/video detectors monitor space status in real time; guidance screens direct drivers to free areas; reverse car-finding terminals let drivers locate vehicles by plate number
  3. Management layer: Cloud platform remotely monitors real-time space availability, revenue and equipment status across multiple lots

Integration Capabilities:

  • Supports linkage with building management and fire protection systems
  • Supports both cloud-managed and locally deployed modes
  • Supports multiple charging modes (by duration/per use/monthly pass/frictionless payment)

Advantages & Delivery

With a microservice-architecture parking cloud platform at its core, AXLTeco provides flexible, accurate and efficient smart parking solutions.

Key Differentiators:

  • License plate recognition rate above 99%, supporting multiple plate formats
  • Flexible choice of cloud management or local deployment
  • Deployed across multiple Lagos commercial complexes and residential communities, running stably for over 24 months
  • Supports linkage with building management systems

Customer Value: Raises parking turnover by more than 30%, cuts labor costs by 60%, and shortens average space hunting time to under 2 minutes.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Solution design → Equipment installation → Platform deployment → System commissioning → Training and handover.

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, remote operations support, continuous platform upgrades.

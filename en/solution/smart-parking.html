Smart Parking Solution

Space Guidance · Unmanned · Mobile Payment

For commercial complexes, residential compounds, transport hubs and roadside parking, a fully unmanned entry-to-exit management solution that addresses the core pain points of low parking turnover, high labor costs and difficult space hunting, achieving unattended operation, smart guidance and frictionless payment.

Solution Overview

Provides fully unmanned entry-to-exit management solutions for commercial complexes, residential compounds, transport hubs and roadside parking, addressing low turnover, high labor costs and difficult space hunting, and achieving unattended operation, smart guidance and frictionless payment.

Applicable to: Commercial complexes, residential compounds, airport/train station parking, roadside parking.

  • Raises parking turnover by more than 30%
  • Cuts labor costs by 60%
  • Average space hunting time shortened to under 2 minutes
  • Running stably for over 24 months across multiple Lagos projects

Business Challenges

In parking lot operations, customers often face peak-hour congestion, rising labor costs and poor driver experience, and urgently need an unmanned, entry-to-exit parking management system.

Slow entry

Manual ticketing and registration take time; queues form at entrances during peaks.

Hard space hunting

Drivers wander looking for free spaces; vehicles circulate chaotically inside.

Long payment queues

Manual exit collection is slow; lines back up badly at peak times.

High management cost

Every exit needs a fee collector; labor is costly and error-prone.

Data transparency

Real-time statistics on space utilization and revenue are needed for operating decisions.

Fully unmanned flow

Recognition, guidance, billing and payment should run automatically end to end.

Typical Scenarios: Mall weekend peaksResidential morning/evening peaksAirport long-stay parkingRoadside temporary parking

Target Roles: Parking operators, property management staff, drivers.

Solution Architecture

Built on a three-tier “entrances - lot interior - management platform” architecture covering plate recognition, space guidance, reverse car finding and automatic billing, with linkage to building management and fire systems and a choice of cloud or local deployment.

Entrances handle recognition, gate lifting and billing; the interior detects spaces in real time and guides drivers; the management layer remotely monitors multiple lots.

Core Modules

Plate recognition

Recognition rate above 99% across multiple plate formats; gates lift automatically on entry.

Guidance & reverse finding

Real-time guidance to free spaces; enter a plate number to find where the car is.

Frictionless payment

Hourly/per-use/monthly billing modes with automatic settlement at exit.

Operating data management

Real-time statistics on space utilization and revenue supporting operating decisions.

Integration Capabilities:

  • Supports linkage with building management and fire protection systems
  • Cloud management or local deployment
  • Multiple billing modes (hourly/per-use/monthly/frictionless payment)

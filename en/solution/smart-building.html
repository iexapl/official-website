Smart Building Solution

Smart building solution with automation, security and energy management.

Provides operators of commercial offices, serviced apartments, hospital buildings and commercial complexes with a BIM+IoT digital twin based intelligent management solution, addressing fragmented management, high energy use and slow response caused by isolated subsystems, and achieving unified building equipment management and coordinated control.

核心能力

IBMS integration

Integrates BAS, security, fire, access control, parking, energy and 20+ subsystems.

BIM+IoT digital twin

BIM and IoT data fused for full life-cycle 3D visualized building management.

AI energy optimization

Algorithms analyze usage patterns and cut building energy use by 15%-25%.

Intelligent fire linkage

Fire alarms automatically release access, switch broadcast, recall elevators and start smoke exhaust.

Tenant mobile services

Visitor booking, meeting room reservation and repair requests in one place.

Benchmark case verified

Successfully deployed and verified at the Lagos Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) project, demonstrating proven solution maturity.

Smart Building Solution

BAS · Energy Management · Smart Security

For operators of commercial offices, serviced apartments, hospital buildings and commercial complexes, a BIM+IoT digital twin based intelligent building management solution that addresses the core pain points of fragmented management, high energy consumption and slow response caused by subsystems running in silos, achieving unified equipment management and coordinated control.

Solution Overview

Provides operators of commercial offices, serviced apartments, hospital buildings and commercial complexes with a BIM+IoT digital twin based intelligent management solution, addressing fragmented management, high energy use and slow response caused by isolated subsystems, and achieving unified building equipment management and coordinated control.

Applicable to: Office towers, serviced apartments, hospitals, commercial complexes.

  • BIM+IoT digital twin technology for full life-cycle visualized building management
  • AI-driven energy optimization strategies can cut building energy use by 15%-25%
  • A unified platform breaks information silos and reduces operations labor costs by more than 30%
  • Successfully deployed in a government office building

Benefits: Higher operations efficiency, lower energy consumption, stronger safety assurance, better tenant experience, and higher property asset value.

Business Challenges

In modern building operations, customers often face subsystem silos, coarse-grained energy management and slow maintenance response, and urgently need a unified, data-connected and intelligently linked building management platform.

Subsystems in silos

BAS, security, fire, access control and parking run independently without shared data.

Coarse energy management

No sub-metering for utilities; usage patterns are unclear and energy-saving measures lack data support.

Slow maintenance response

Faults are found through manual inspection; reactive repairs are costly.

Information silos inflate operating cost

Fragmented management keeps energy bills high and tenant experience poor; operating cost and asset value both suffer.

Unified integrated management

One platform should connect subsystem data and cross-system linkage.

Data-driven energy saving

AI algorithms should analyze energy patterns and produce optimization strategies.

Typical Scenarios: Daily office building operationsHotel guest-room servicesHospital operating-room environment monitoringMulti-format complex management

Target Roles: Property managers, operations engineers, security directors, administrative staff.

Solution Architecture

Built on a three-layer “building equipment - building management - tenant services” architecture centered on an IBMS integration platform, covering equipment monitoring, energy optimization and tenant services, and integrating more than 20 subsystems including BAS, security, fire, access control, parking and energy.

Sensors and controllers on the equipment layer collect building data; the IBMS platform monitors equipment, energy and security and produces AI optimization strategies; the service layer gives tenants and visitors mobile services.

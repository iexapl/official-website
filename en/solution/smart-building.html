Smart Building Solution

BAS · Security · Fire · Energy · Visitor Management

Solution Architecture

Technical Foundation: BIM+IoT digital twin technology supporting local deployment.

[Flow] Sensor data collection → edge gateway aggregation → AI energy optimization → automatic temperature and lighting adjustment → equipment anomaly alerts → maintenance work orders → mobile dispatch.

[User End] Tenants use mobile APP or WeChat mini-programs for visitor booking, meeting room reservation, and repair requests; visitors pass through gates via QR code or face recognition with automatic elevator dispatch.

[Equipment Layer] Sensors and controllers in HVAC rooms, electrical rooms, and elevator machine rooms; lighting control and AC panels in public areas; access control, cameras, and parking guidance at entrances and garages.

[Management Center] Located in the property office, monitoring building equipment status, energy consumption, and security alerts through a unified platform.

This solution uses IBMS as the core platform, with a three-layer "Equipment- Management-User Service" architecture.

Solution Components

Building Automation (BA)
Automatic monitoring and optimized control of HVAC, lighting, elevators, and water supply/drainage.
Security System
Integrated management of video surveillance, access control, intrusion alarms, and electronic patrol.
Fire System
Automatic fire alarm, fire linkage control, emergency broadcast, and evacuation guidance.
Energy Management
Sub-metering, energy efficiency analysis, and energy-saving control strategies to reduce operating costs.
Visitor Management
Appointment registration, ID verification, elevator linkage, and visitor trajectory tracking.
Smart Parking
License plate recognition, parking guidance, reverse vehicle finding, and contactless payment.

Application Scenarios

Commercial Offices

Automatic environmental control in public areas, tenant energy sub-metering, and self-service visitor access.

Hotels & Apartments

Integrated room intelligent control, energy management, security surveillance, and service robot dispatching.

Hospital Buildings

Operating room environment monitoring, medical equipment asset management, ward call linkage, and energy optimization.

Commercial Complexes

Unified multi-tenant management platform with passenger flow analysis, equipment linkage, and emergency evacuation command.

Solution Advantages

Based on BIM+IoT digital twin technology, the solution enables lifecycle visual management of buildings. AI-driven energy optimization strategies can reduce building energy consumption by 15%-25%. The unified platform breaks information silos and reduces O&M labor costs by over 30%.

Solution Value

Improve building operational efficiency, reduce energy consumption, enhance security, improve tenant experience, and increase property asset value.

AXLTeco deployed a complete intelligent building management system at the FIRS building in Lagos, covering building automation, security surveillance, and fire linkage subsystems, providing a benchmark case for intelligent operations in large government office buildings.

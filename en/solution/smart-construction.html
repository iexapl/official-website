Smart Construction Site Solution

Safety · Quality · Progress · Environment · Personnel

Solution Architecture

Technical Foundation: Microservices-based unified management platform supporting local and cloud deployment.

[Flow] Face scan entry → attendance sync to command center → site data aggregation via edge gateway → automatic alert push to managers → closed-loop confirmation after rectification.

[Workface] Workers receive tasks and report progress via mobile APP; safety officers record inspections with photos; tower crane operators have real-time load monitoring terminals.

[Project Site] Face recognition access control and license plate recognition barriers at site entrances; sensors and AI cameras on tower cranes, deep excavations, and high-formwork areas; environmental monitoring stations for dust, noise, and weather data.

[Command Center] Located at project headquarters or the client office, real-time monitoring of construction progress, safety alerts, attendance, and environmental data through large-screen dashboards and PC terminals.

This solution adopts a three-tier "Command Center-Project Site-Workface" management architecture, covering six management dimensions: personnel, safety, quality, progress, materials, and environment.

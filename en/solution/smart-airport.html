Smart Airport Solution

Passenger Service · Security · Operations

For airport operators and aviation authorities, a full-scenario intelligent solution covering passenger services, security, operations and energy management that addresses the core pain points of low passenger processing efficiency, manual-dependent security screening and suboptimal resource utilization, improving passenger satisfaction and airport operational efficiency.

Solution Overview

Provides airport operators and aviation authorities with full-scenario intelligent solutions covering passenger services, security, operations and energy management, addressing low passenger processing efficiency, manual-dependent screening and suboptimal resource utilization, and improving passenger satisfaction and operational efficiency.

Applicable to: Civil airports, cargo airports, terminals, aprons, cargo areas.

  • Compliant with ICAO safety standards, with multi-language and multi-currency support
  • Modular design allows phased construction, reducing one-off investment risk
  • Self-service check-in/bag drop/boarding cuts queuing time
  • Intelligent stand allocation and ground vehicle dispatching optimize resource utilization

Benefits: Higher passenger processing efficiency, stronger safety assurance, optimized airport resource utilization, and increased commercial revenue.

Business Challenges

In airport operations, customers often face low passenger processing efficiency, manual-dependent screening and complex resource coordination, and urgently need a full-scenario intelligent system built to ICAO standards, deployable in phases, with unified data convergence.

Low processing efficiency

Long queues at check-in, screening and boarding hurt the passenger experience.

Manual-dependent screening

Manual image review is slow and inconsistent, creating security risk.

Flight info out of sync

Subsystem silos delay flight updates reaching passengers and staff.

Unbalanced ground resources

Stand allocation and vehicle dispatching rely on experience; peak-hour shortages coexist with idle capacity, pressuring turnaround efficiency.

Self-service passenger flow

Self-service check-in, bag drop and boarding are needed to cut queues.

Intelligent resource scheduling

Smart stand allocation and ground vehicle dispatching are needed to balance utilization.

Typical Scenarios: Peak-hour high-volume processingMulti-currency settlement for international flightsCargo area trackingFlight delay contingency

Target Roles: Airport operations staff, screening officers, ground handlers, passenger service staff.

Solution Architecture

Built on a “terminal-apron-cargo area” collaborative architecture covering passenger services, security screening, cargo management and AOC operations control, with multi-language and multi-currency support and real-time data convergence across subsystems.

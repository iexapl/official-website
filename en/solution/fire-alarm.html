Fire Alarm System Solution

Early Warning · Linkage Control · Evacuation Guidance

For commercial buildings, residential communities, industrial plants, warehousing and logistics facilities, hospitals, schools and all kinds of buildings, an automatic fire alarm and intelligent linkage control solution that addresses the core pain points of delayed fire detection, slow linkage response and difficult fire compliance management, achieving early fire warning and full-chain intelligent linkage.

Solution Overview

Provides automatic fire alarm and intelligent linkage control solutions for all kinds of buildings, addressing delayed fire detection, disconnected linkage response and difficult compliance management, and achieving early fire warning and full-chain intelligent linkage.

Applicable to: Commercial buildings, residential communities, industrial plants, warehousing and logistics, hospitals, schools.

  • High-sensitivity detectors enable early fire warning
  • Intelligent algorithms reduce false alarm rates
  • Strong linkage control capability automatically executes fire response plans
  • Supports networked supervision and connection to city remote fire monitoring platforms

Benefits: Buys valuable time for evacuation, effectively contains fire spread, and meets fire compliance requirements.

Business Challenges

In building fire safety management, delayed fire detection, disconnected linkage response and difficult compliance supervision are long-standing hazards.

  • Delayed fire detection: Traditional manual patrols cannot cover 24 hours, and early-stage fires are easily overlooked
  • Disconnected linkage response: After an alarm, each fire subsystem must be started manually one by one, missing the best response window
  • Frequent false alarms: Traditional detectors are sensitive to dust and steam; high false alarm rates create a crying-wolf effect
  • Difficult compliance supervision: Fire equipment inspection records are scattered, and complete ledgers are hard to produce quickly during government inspections

Typical Scenarios: Dense-traffic areas of commercial complexes during business hours, unattended industrial plant areas at night, hospital wards, school dormitories.

Intended Users: Fire duty staff, property management staff, safety directors, government fire inspectors.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a distributed detect-alarm-linkage-management architecture for early fire detection and intelligent linkage control.

  1. Detection layer: Photoelectric smoke, heat, flame and combustible gas detectors, plus manual alarm buttons
  2. Control layer: Fire alarm control panels for signal processing and zone confirmation
  3. Linkage layer: Automatic activation of sprinklers, smoke exhaust, fire shutter descent, emergency lighting, fire broadcast, access control release and elevator recall
  4. Management layer: Graphic display units showing fire locations at a glance, with support for networked supervision

Integration Capabilities:

  • Linked with sprinkler, smoke exhaust, fire shutter, emergency lighting, access control and broadcast systems
  • Supports connection to city remote fire monitoring platforms
  • Linked with building management systems to switch to emergency mode automatically during fires

Advantages & Delivery

With high-sensitivity detection and intelligent linkage at its core, AXLTeco provides automatic fire alarm systems that meet international fire standards.

Key Differentiators:

  • High-sensitivity detectors enable early fire warning
  • Intelligent algorithms effectively reduce false alarm rates
  • After alarm confirmation, the full-chain fire response plan executes automatically without manual intervention
  • Graphical display makes fire locations clear at a glance

Customer Value: Detects fires and raises alarms immediately, buying valuable time for evacuation; automatic equipment linkage effectively contains fire spread.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Solution design → Equipment supply → Installation and wiring → System commissioning → Fire acceptance inspection → Training and handover.

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, support for fire acceptance inspection, periodic inspection and maintenance.

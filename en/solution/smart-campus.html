Smart Campus Solution

Campus Security · Academic Management · IoT Control

For schools of all levels from kindergartens to universities, a four-in-one smart campus solution spanning security, teaching, logistics and home-school interaction that addresses the core pain points of campus safety hazards, chaotic teaching resource management, inefficient logistics services and poor home-school communication, building a safe, convenient and intelligent campus environment.

Solution Overview

Provides schools of all levels with a four-in-one smart campus solution spanning security, teaching, logistics and home-school interaction, addressing safety hazards, chaotic resource management, inefficient logistics and poor home-school communication, and building a safe, convenient and intelligent campus.

Applicable to: Kindergartens, primary and secondary schools, universities, vocational schools, training institutions.

  • Modular design flexibly configured to school size
  • Unified platform lowers multi-system maintenance costs
  • Open APIs for integration with existing academic systems
  • Successfully deployed at multiple Nigerian schools

Benefits: Higher campus safety management, optimized teaching resource allocation, lower energy consumption, and greater satisfaction for students and staff.

Business Challenges

In daily school management, customers often face safety hazards, difficult resource allocation and inefficient logistics, and urgently need a smart campus system on a unified platform with open APIs and flexible configuration for any school size.

Safety hazards

Weak perimeter defense, easy entry by strangers and coarse management of student entry and exit.

Chaotic teaching resource management

Multimedia equipment status unknown, classroom comfort uncontrollable, attendance taken by hand.

Inefficient logistics

Paper-based dormitory registration, slow canteen settlement, lengthy repair workflows.

Home-school information gap

Parents cannot track arrivals; school notices reach few people; both emergency response and daily communication suffer.

Full-scenario safety defense

Access control, surveillance, perimeter defense and one-touch alarms should form a closed loop.

Real-time home-school link

Student entries and exits should notify parents automatically; campus updates should reach them precisely.

Typical Scenarios: Student arrival and departure managementClassroom attendance statisticsDormitory late-return managementCampus emergency response

Target Roles: School leadership, teachers, security staff, logistics staff, parents.

Solution Architecture

Built on a four-in-one “campus security - teaching management - logistics services - home-school interaction” architecture covering access and attendance, smart classrooms, dormitory and canteen services and parent communication, on a unified platform with API integration to academic systems.

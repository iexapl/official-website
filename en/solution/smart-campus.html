Smart Campus Solution

Security · Attendance & Access Control Equipment · Access · Energy · Information

Solution Architecture

Technical Foundation: Microservices-based campus digital platform supporting modular configuration.

[Flow] Face scan entry → attendance sync to academic system → parent notification → automatic class attendance → real-time consumption sync → anomaly alerts to security.

[Parent End] Mobile APP for arrival/departure notifications, attendance records, online leave requests, and fee payment.

[Teaching Area] Smart environmental controls in classrooms; remote control of projectors, audio, and curtains in multimedia rooms.

[Gate/Dormitory] Face recognition access control; stranger intrusion alerts with camera tracking; electronic class boards and payment terminals in dormitory areas.

[Admin Center] Located in the school administration building or security room, unified management of security, academics, logistics, and assets.

This solution adopts a four-in-one "Security-Teaching-Logistics-Home-School" architecture for a safe, convenient, and intelligent campus environment.

Solution Components

Access Control
Face recognition/card entry, zone-based permission control, and visitor appointment management.
CCTV System
Full campus coverage with intelligent analysis in key areas and real-time abnormal event alerts.
Campus Broadcasting
Zone-based broadcasting, scheduled playback, and emergency broadcast with multimedia support.
One-Card System
Unified card for access, payment, attendance, and library services.
Energy Management
Real-time monitoring of water, electricity, and gas with consumption analysis and energy-saving recommendations.
Visitor Management
Online appointment, ID verification, trajectory tracking, and departure confirmation.

Application Scenarios

Security & Prevention

Campus perimeter protection, key-area monitoring, stranger alerts, and one-click alarm linkage.

Teaching Management

Smart classroom environment control, multimedia device management, and classroom attendance statistics.

Logistics Services

Dormitory access control, cafeteria consumption tracking, maintenance work order workflow, and energy-saving control.

Home-School Communication

Student entry/exit notifications, visitor appointment review, and campus activity push.

Solution Advantages

The system adopts a modular design that can be flexibly configured according to school scale, supporting tiered deployment from kindergartens to large universities. A unified platform reduces multi-system maintenance costs, and open APIs enable seamless integration with existing academic systems.

Solution Value

Improve campus safety management, optimize teaching resource allocation, reduce energy consumption, and create a safe, convenient, and comfortable learning and living environment for students and faculty.

Project Practice

AXLTeco has successfully deployed smart campus solutions in multiple schools across Nigeria, covering access control, video surveillance, and broadcast notification subsystems, significantly improving campus safety management and operational efficiency.

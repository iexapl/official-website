Smart Campus Solution

Smart campus solution with security, education management and IoT control.

Provides schools of all levels with a four-in-one smart campus solution spanning security, teaching, logistics and home-school interaction, addressing safety hazards, chaotic resource management, inefficient logistics and poor home-school communication, and building a safe, convenient and intelligent campus.

Business Challenges

Chaotic teaching resource management

Multimedia equipment status unknown, classroom comfort uncontrollable, attendance taken by hand.

Business Impact

Safety hazards

Weak perimeter defense, easy entry by strangers and coarse management of student entry and exit.

Safety and teaching experience eroded

Lagging perimeter, access and attendance information slows incident response and undermines parent trust and campus efficiency.

Business Requirements

Inefficient logistics

Paper-based dormitory registration, slow canteen settlement, lengthy repair workflows.

Home-school information gap

Parents cannot track arrivals; school notices reach few people; both emergency response and daily communication suffer.

Full-scenario safety defense

Access control, surveillance, perimeter defense and one-touch alarms should form a closed loop.

Real-time home-school link

Student entries and exits should notify parents automatically; campus updates should reach them precisely.

Core Capabilities

Campus security system

Face-recognition access, full-coverage surveillance, perimeter defense and one-touch alarm linkage.

Smart classrooms

Environment control, multimedia device management and attendance statistics.

Smart dormitory management

Access management and late-return statistics with automatic alerts on anomalies.

Canteen &amp; logistics

Spending statistics, repair ticket workflow and energy control.

Home-school interaction

Entry/exit notifications, visitor booking approval and campus updates for parents.

Solution Architecture

Advantages & Delivery

With modular design and unified platform capability, we support higher campus safety and better teaching and logistics efficiency, delivered through module-by-module implementation, system integration, training and vacation-period maintenance.

Differentiated Advantages

Modular Flexible Selection

Tiered deployment from kindergartens to universities; select modules on demand for flexible investment.

Unified Platform

Security, teaching and logistics integrated in one platform, avoiding silos and cutting maintenance cost.

Open API Integration

Seamless connection to existing academic systems protects prior investment with shared data.

Large-Project Delivery Experience

Backed by intelligent-system delivery on large projects such as the Mile 2 transport hub — a mature, reliable delivery path for campus scenarios.

Holiday-Window Maintenance

Centralized maintenance in summer and winter breaks without disturbing teaching.

Safety and Learning Together

Face-recognition access secures the campus; multimedia systems improve teaching quality.

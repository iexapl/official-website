Smart Traffic Solution

Signal Control · Flow Monitoring · Violation Capture

For urban traffic management authorities and transport infrastructure builders, a full-scenario intelligent traffic management solution covering signal control, video surveillance, violation detection, flow analysis and guidance publishing that addresses the core pain points of severe intersection congestion, frequent traffic violations and underutilized traffic data, improving road capacity and traffic safety.

Solution Overview

Provides urban traffic management authorities and transport infrastructure builders with a full-scenario intelligent traffic management solution covering signal control, video surveillance, violation detection, flow analysis and guidance publishing, addressing severe intersection congestion, frequent violations and underutilized traffic data, and improving road capacity and traffic safety.

Applicable to: Urban traffic management departments, transport infrastructure builders, highway operators.

  • Compatible with mainstream domestic and international signal controller brands, protecting existing investments
  • AI video analytics require no pavement-cutting loops, shortening construction periods
  • Open data interfaces support connection to city traffic command centers and map service providers
  • Participated in the Lagos Mile 2, Light Rail Blue Line and Kano railway station projects

Benefits: Higher road capacity, fewer traffic accidents, and better public transport services.

Business Challenges

In urban traffic management, normalized intersection congestion, frequent violations and weak public transport service are the three chronic ills constraining city efficiency.

  • Severe intersection congestion: Fixed signal timing cannot adapt to flow changes, causing long peak-hour queues
  • Frequent violations: Red-light running, speeding and wrong-way driving rely on manual enforcement with limited coverage
  • Dormant traffic data: Intersection flow data is underused, leaving optimization decisions without data support
  • Weak public transport: Buses get no priority passage, punctuality is low and the passenger experience is poor

Typical Scenarios: Morning and evening peak signal optimization, automatic violation capture, real-time traffic information publishing, bus priority passage.

Intended Users: Traffic engineers, traffic police command staff, road maintenance staff.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a three-tier architecture of intersection sensing, area coordination and central control, achieving real-time flow monitoring and signal optimization.

  1. Intersection layer: Video detectors, radar detectors, signal controllers, AI edge analysis units and automated enforcement cameras
  2. Road segment layer: Guidance screens publishing real-time traffic information; bus priority detection automatically extends green phases
  3. Central layer: The signal control platform shows real-time flow, signal status and congestion index per intersection for remote adjustment

Integration Capabilities:

  • Compatible with mainstream domestic and international signal controller brands
  • AI video analytics without pavement cutting
  • Open data interfaces support connection to city traffic command centers and map service providers

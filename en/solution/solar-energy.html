Solar Energy Storage Solution

Solar energy storage solution with generation, storage and smart dispatching.

Provides integrated PV generation and energy storage green energy solutions for commercial and industrial rooftops, factory parks, off-grid villages, microgrids and telecom base stations, addressing unstable power supply, high electricity costs and large carbon emissions in Africa, and delivering clean and reliable green energy supply and energy independence.

Business Challenges

Unstable power supply

Frequent outages disrupt operations; backup generators burn costly fuel and are noisy.

High electricity costs

Commercial and industrial tariffs are high with big peak-valley gaps; power takes a large share of operating spend.

Single-source energy mix

Heavy dependence on grid and diesel; clean energy takes a small share.

Off-grid areas lack power

Remote villages and base stations have no stable supply.

Business Impact

Outages and tariffs erode margins

Frequent outages interrupt operations while fuel backup costs climb; high tariffs and unstable supply steadily eat into business results and daily life.

Business Requirements

PV + storage integration

Generation and storage must work together for self-consumption and backup.

Visible, controllable energy

Generation, storage, consumption and revenue should be monitored with data.

Core Capabilities

PV generation

High-efficiency rooftop/ground modules with conversion efficiency verified at a high level in field tests.

Energy storage

Modular lithium battery packs that scale flexibly, with thermal control for service life.

EMS energy management

Intelligent algorithms optimize charge/discharge to maximize self-consumption.

PV-storage-charging integration

EV chargers supplied first by clean power.

Remote monitoring

Generation, storage, consumption and revenue visible in real time with remote operations.

Solution Architecture

Advantages & Delivery

With intelligent EMS algorithms and modular PV-storage design, we deliver energy value — lower bills and reliable supply — through site survey, grid connection acceptance and long-term warranty-backed operations.

Differentiated Advantages

High-Yield Generation

High-efficiency PV modules with field-proven conversion efficiency generate more per square meter.

Intelligent Dispatch

Smart EMS algorithms maximize self-consumption and optimize peak-valley arbitrage returns.

Flexible Expansion

Modular storage scales smoothly with business growth; phased investment keeps risk controllable.

Dual-Mode Operation

Grid-tied and off-grid modes with automatic backup switchoever deliver high supply reliability.

Remote Monitoring & O&M

Real-time remote view of PV power, storage status and consumption keeps O&M easy.

All-Scenario Fit

Serves factories, commercial buildings, off-grid villages and telecom towers for green power and backup.

Solar & Storage Solution

PV Generation · Energy Storage · Smart Grid

For commercial and industrial rooftops, factory parks, off-grid villages, microgrids and telecom base stations, an integrated PV generation and energy storage green energy solution that addresses the core pain points of unstable power supply, high electricity costs and large carbon emissions in Africa, delivering clean and reliable green energy supply and energy independence.

Solution Overview

Provides integrated PV generation and energy storage green energy solutions for commercial and industrial rooftops, factory parks, off-grid villages, microgrids and telecom base stations, addressing unstable power supply, high electricity costs and large carbon emissions in Africa, and delivering clean and reliable green energy supply and energy independence.

Applicable to: Factory parks, commercial buildings, off-grid villages, telecom base stations, microgrids.

  • High-efficiency PV modules with verified conversion efficiency
  • Intelligent EMS algorithms maximize self-consumption ratio
  • Modular energy storage design for flexible expansion
  • Supports both grid-tied and off-grid operation modes

Benefits: Lower electricity bills, reduced carbon emissions, relieved grid pressure, and reliable backup power in areas with unstable supply.

Business Challenges

Operating in Africa's energy environment, customers often face unstable power supply, high electricity costs and a single-source energy mix, and urgently need a green energy solution integrating generation, storage and management.

Unstable power supply

Frequent outages disrupt operations; backup generators burn costly fuel and are noisy.

High electricity costs

Commercial and industrial tariffs are high with big peak-valley gaps; power takes a large share of operating spend.

Single-source energy mix

Heavy dependence on grid and diesel; clean energy takes a small share.

Off-grid areas lack power

Remote villages and base stations have no stable supply.

PV + storage integration

Generation and storage must work together for self-consumption and backup.

Visible, controllable energy

Generation, storage, consumption and revenue should be monitored with data.

Typical Scenarios: Daytime factory productionCommercial peak-valley arbitrageOff-grid village standalone supplyBase station backup power

Target Roles: Enterprise energy managers, facility operations staff, park operators, base station maintenance staff.

Solution Architecture

Built on a four-layer “generation-storage-management-consumption” architecture covering PV generation, battery storage and energy management, supporting both grid-tied sales and off-grid standalone operation plus EV-charger integration.

The generation layer converts DC to AC; the storage layer manages charging and discharging via BMS and PCS; the management layer optimizes dispatch via EMS; the consumption layer prioritizes local use, sends surplus to the grid and connects chargers.

Core Modules

PV generation

High-efficiency rooftop/ground modules with conversion efficiency verified at a high level in field tests.

Energy storage

Modular lithium battery packs that scale flexibly, with thermal control for service life.

EMS energy management

PV-storage-charging integration

Remote monitoring

Integration Capabilities:

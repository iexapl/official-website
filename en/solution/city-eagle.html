City Eagle Eye Solution

City eagle eye solution using aerial monitoring and AI for urban security.

Provides urban public security authorities and operators of large transport hubs with a three-dimensional defense solution, addressing insufficient surveillance coverage in key areas, slow incident response and low manual monitoring efficiency, and achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and a rapid response closed loop.

Business Challenges

Insufficient coverage

Ordinary cameras cover little per unit; urban vantage points and large areas leave many blind spots.

Low manual monitoring efficiency

Massive video feeds watched by people lead to fatigue and missed events.

Long response chains

From spotting an anomaly to dispatching response, information passes through many slow links.

Business Requirements

No vertical view hampers response

Ground-level cameras cannot see from above; large events and emergencies lack an overall scene view, slowing decisions.

Wide-area high-altitude watch

A handful of devices should cover key areas with a radius of several kilometers.

AI alerting with fast closed loop

Anomalies should be detected and located automatically and linked to response resources.

Core Capabilities

High-altitude watch

Long-zoom PTZ and 360° panoramic cameras on urban vantage points; one unit covers a 3-5 km radius.

AI recognition

Automatic detection of crowd gathering, illegal parking and fire smoke, with alarm accuracy above 95%.

Drone-linked patrol

Drones take off automatically after alerts and stream live video from the scene.

Alert map &amp; dispatch

Alerts plotted on the map, verified on the video wall, response resources dispatched in one click.

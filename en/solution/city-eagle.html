City Eagle Eye Solution

High-Altitude Surveillance · AI Recognition · 3D Defense

For urban public security authorities and operators of large transport hubs, a three-dimensional defense solution of high-altitude watch, low-point gap filling and aerial patrol that addresses the core pain points of insufficient surveillance coverage in key urban areas, slow incident response and low efficiency of manual monitoring, achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and a rapid response closed loop.

Solution Overview

Provides urban public security authorities and operators of large transport hubs with a three-dimensional defense solution, addressing insufficient surveillance coverage in key areas, slow incident response and low manual monitoring efficiency, and achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and a rapid response closed loop.

Applicable to: Key urban areas, large event venues, transport hubs, forest fire prevention zones, coastlines.

  • A single high-altitude watch camera covers a radius of 3-5 km, equivalent to dozens of ordinary cameras
  • AI analytics replace manual monitoring, with alarm accuracy above 95%
  • Linked with drone systems to achieve a detect-locate-respond rapid closed loop

Benefits: Lower security operations costs, shorter incident response time, and higher urban safety coverage density.

Business Challenges

In urban public security management, customers often face insufficient camera coverage, low manual-monitoring efficiency and long response chains, and urgently need a three-dimensional defense system with wide-area coverage, automatic AI alerts and rapid coordinated response.

Pain

Insufficient coverage

Ordinary cameras cover little per unit; urban vantage points and large areas leave many blind spots.

Pain

Low manual monitoring efficiency

Massive video feeds watched by people lead to fatigue and missed events.

Pain

Long response chains

From spotting an anomaly to dispatching response, information passes through many slow links.

Impact

No vertical view hampers response

Ground-level cameras cannot see from above; large events and emergencies lack an overall scene view, slowing decisions.

Need

Wide-area high-altitude watch

A handful of devices should cover key areas with a radius of several kilometers.

Need

AI alerting with fast closed loop

Anomalies should be detected and located automatically and linked to response resources.

Typical Scenarios: Crowd gathering watch in urban plazasIllegal parking detectionFire smoke early warningLarge event security

Target Roles: Public security command center staff, security managers, emergency dispatchers.

Solution Architecture

City Eagle Eye follows a five-layer business topology: Police Command, Investigation and other offices run daily applications such as Video Patrol and Tracking, coordinated by the platform layer; edge devices like High-Point Cam and Street Dome sit on site, linking perception devices such as Tower View and Face Capture — data flows upward and commands flow downward across the full chain.

Business layout: the user layer holds management offices (Police Command, Investigation…); the application layer carries daily functions; the platform layer provides Video Network and Image Store; the edge and network layer marks on-site equipment positions (High-Point Cam, Street Dome…); perception devices are deployed at Tower View, Face Capture and other field locations.

Core Modules

High-altitude watch

Long-zoom PTZ and 360° panoramic cameras on urban vantage points; one unit covers a 3-5 km radius.

AI recognition

Automatic detection of crowd gathering, illegal parking and fire smoke, with alarm accuracy above 95%.

Drone-linked patrol

Drones take off automatically after alerts and stream live video from the scene.

Alert map & dispatch

Alerts plotted on the map, verified on the video wall, response resources dispatched in one click.

Integration Capabilities:

  • Connects to public security command platforms for a detect-assess-dispatch-respond loop
  • AI recognition of crowd gathering, illegal parking, fire smoke and other anomalies
  • Drone linkage: automatic takeoff to the scene for supplementary reconnaissance

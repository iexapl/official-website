City Eagle Eye Solution

High-Altitude Surveillance · AI Recognition · 3D Defense

For urban public security authorities and operators of large transport hubs, a three-dimensional defense solution of high-altitude watch, low-point gap filling and aerial patrol that addresses the core pain points of insufficient surveillance coverage in key urban areas, slow incident response and low efficiency of manual monitoring, achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and a rapid response closed loop.

Solution Overview

Provides urban public security authorities and operators of large transport hubs with a three-dimensional defense solution, addressing insufficient surveillance coverage in key areas, slow incident response and low manual monitoring efficiency, and achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and a rapid response closed loop.

Applicable to: Key urban areas, large event venues, transport hubs, forest fire prevention zones, coastlines.

  • A single high-altitude watch camera covers a radius of 3-5 km, equivalent to dozens of ordinary cameras
  • AI analytics replace manual monitoring, with alarm accuracy above 95%
  • Linked with drone systems to achieve a detect-locate-respond rapid closed loop

Benefits: Lower security operations costs, shorter incident response time, and higher urban safety coverage density.

Business Challenges

In urban public security management, traditional video surveillance suffers from large blind spots, slow incident detection and delayed response.

  • Insufficient surveillance coverage: Ordinary cameras cover a small area per unit, leaving many blind spots at urban high points and over large areas
  • Low manual monitoring efficiency: Massive video feeds rely on human watching, with high fatigue and high miss rates
  • Long incident response chains: From anomaly detection to dispatch involves many steps and slow information transfer
  • Missing aerial perspective: Ground surveillance cannot cover aerial views, leaving large events and emergencies without three-dimensional awareness

Typical Scenarios: Crowd gathering monitoring in urban squares, illegal parking detection, fire and smoke early warning, security for large events.

Intended Users: Police command center staff, security management staff, emergency dispatch staff.

Solution Architecture

Builds a three-layer three-dimensional defense network of high-altitude watch, low-point gap filling and aerial patrol, achieving panoramic AI video monitoring and rapid response in key areas.

  1. High-altitude layer: Long-zoom PTZ cameras and 360-degree panoramic cameras deployed at urban high points, each covering a radius of 3-5 km
  2. Low-point layer: Bullet cameras, dome cameras, face recognition checkpoints and vehicle checkpoints along roads, squares and entrances
  3. Aerial layer: Drone docks deployed to take off automatically toward the scene for supplementary reconnaissance upon alarm
  4. Command center: Real-time video walls and alarm maps on large screens to dispatch response resources

Integration Capabilities:

  • Connected with police command platforms for detect-assess-dispatch-respond rapid handling
  • AI recognition of crowd gathering, illegal parking, fire and smoke anomalies
  • Linked with drone systems for automatic takeoff toward the scene

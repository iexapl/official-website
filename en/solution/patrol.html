Smart Patrol Solution

Electronic Patrol · Track Trace · Hazard Report

For residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses, hospitals and other venues requiring security patrols, a digital guard tour management solution that addresses the core pain points of uncontrollable patrol execution, slow anomaly reporting and unmeasurable patrol quality, achieving full-process digital patrol management and a security linkage closed loop.

Solution Overview

Provides digital guard tour management solutions for residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses and hospitals, addressing uncontrollable patrol execution, slow anomaly reporting and unmeasurable patrol quality, and achieving full-process digital patrol management and a security linkage closed loop.

Applicable to: Residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses, hospitals.

  • Multiple check-in methods (NFC/QR code/Bluetooth/GPS) for different scenarios
  • Real-time positioning and track playback to keep patrol status visible
  • Instant anomaly reporting and push notifications for rapid response
  • Linked with video surveillance for visualized patrol management

Benefits: Eliminates missed-patrol risk, speeds up security response, and optimizes patrol routes and staffing.

Business Challenges

In property and park security management, traditional manual patrols suffer from uncontrollable execution, slow anomaly detection and unmeasurable quality.

  • Uncontrollable patrol execution: Whether guards follow routes and reach checkpoints on time is hard to verify
  • Slow anomaly reporting: On-site findings are reported by phone or walkie-talkie, with slow and loss-prone information transfer
  • Unmeasurable patrol quality: Without data support, patrol effectiveness cannot be assessed or routes optimized
  • Disconnected from surveillance: Patrols and video surveillance run separately without coordination

Typical Scenarios: Night-time park patrols, pre- and post-opening inspections of commercial complexes, campus safety patrols, key-area inspections in hospitals.

Intended Users: Security guards, property managers, security supervisors, operations staff.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a four-layer architecture of checkpoints, mobile terminals, management platform and linked response, achieving digital patrol management and a security linkage closed loop.

  1. Checkpoint layer: NFC tags, QR codes, Bluetooth beacons or GPS geofences at key locations
  2. Patrol terminal layer: Mobile app or dedicated devices for QR check-in, with photo evidence, voice notes and GPS tracks
  3. Management platform layer: View patrol routes, real-time positions and anomaly events, and dispatch responses
  4. Linkage layer: Anomalies automatically trigger CCTV video verification, access control linkage and alarm host activation

Integration Capabilities:

  • Linked with video surveillance: check-ins automatically pull up nearby camera feeds
  • Anomalies automatically generate repair work orders
  • Offline mode caches data locally and syncs automatically once the network recovers

Advantages & Delivery

With the mobile patrol management platform at its core, AXLTeco provides electronic guard tour solutions featuring multiple check-in methods, real-time positioning and instant response.

Key Differentiators:

  • Four check-in methods (NFC/QR/Bluetooth/GPS) fitting different scenarios
  • Real-time positioning and track playback give full visibility of patrol status
  • Instant anomaly reporting with automatic linked video verification
  • Comprehensive statistics that quantify patrol quality

Customer Value: Ensures patrols run as planned and eliminates missed-patrol risk; speeds up security response and reduces safety incident rates.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Checkpoint planning → Equipment deployment → Platform configuration → Staff training → Go-live.

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, remote technical support, continuous platform upgrades.

Electronic Patrol Solution

Electronic Patrol · Track Trace · Hazard Report

For residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses, hospitals and other venues requiring security patrols, a digital guard tour management solution that addresses the core pain points of uncontrollable patrol execution, slow anomaly reporting and unmeasurable patrol quality, achieving full-process digital patrol management and a security linkage closed loop.

Solution Overview

Provides digital guard tour management solutions for residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses and hospitals, addressing uncontrollable patrol execution, slow anomaly reporting and unmeasurable patrol quality, and achieving full-process digital patrol management and a security linkage closed loop.

Applicable to: Residential compounds, industrial parks, commercial complexes, campuses, hospitals.

  • Multiple check-in methods (NFC/QR code/Bluetooth/GPS) for different scenarios
  • Real-time positioning and track playback to keep patrol status visible
  • Instant anomaly reporting and push notifications for rapid response
  • Linked with video surveillance for visualized patrol management

Benefits: Eliminates missed-patrol risk, speeds up security response, and optimizes patrol routes and staffing.

Business Challenges

Managing security in compounds, parks and venues, customers often face unverifiable patrol execution, slow anomaly reporting and unmeasurable quality, and urgently need digital guard tour management with verifiable routes, instant reporting and video linkage.

Unverifiable patrol execution

Whether guards follow routes and reach checkpoints on time cannot be verified.

Slow anomaly reporting

Issues found on patrol are reported by phone or radio — slow and easily missed.

Unmeasurable patrol quality

Without data, patrol effectiveness cannot be evaluated or routes optimized.

People and technology disconnected

Patrols and video surveillance run separately; missed-patrol risk and safety hazards persist.

Multi-method checkpoints

NFC/QR/Bluetooth/GPS check-ins are needed to fit different environments.

Patrol-video linkage

Check-ins should pull up nearby cameras automatically, and anomalies should trigger linked verification.

Typical Scenarios: Night-time park patrolsPre/post-opening sweeps of commercial complexesCampus safety sweepsHospital key-area inspection

Target Roles: Security guards, property managers, security directors, operations staff.

Solution Architecture

Built on a four-layer “checkpoints-mobile terminals-management platform-linked response” architecture covering point check-in, real-time positioning, anomaly reporting and video linkage, with support for linkage with video surveillance, access control and alarm systems plus offline data sync.

NFC tags, QR codes, beacons or geofences mark checkpoints; patrol devices check in and record tracks; the platform tracks patrols live; anomalies automatically trigger video verification and ticket-based handling.

Core Modules

Multi-method check-in

NFC/QR/Bluetooth/GPS — four methods covering indoor and outdoor scenarios.

Live positioning & track playback

Patrol status visible at a glance, every check-in traceable.

Instant anomaly reporting

Photo evidence and voice notes pushed to responsible staff immediately.

Linked video verification

Check-ins pull up nearby camera views; anomalies are verified automatically.

Patrol quality statistics

Missed patrols, on-time rates and anomaly handling quantified for assessment.

Integration Capabilities:

  • Linked with video surveillance: check-ins automatically pull up nearby camera views
  • Anomalies automatically create repair tickets
  • Offline data cached locally and synced automatically once the network recovers

Advantages & Delivery

With a mobile guard tour management platform and video linkage capability, we support reliable patrol execution and faster security response, delivered through checkpoint planning, equipment deployment, platform configuration and staff training.

Differentiating Advantages

Four check-in methods

Visible patrol status

Real-time positioning and track playback make execution clear.

Instant anomaly response

Customer Value: Patrols run as planned, eliminating missed-patrol risk; security response is faster, reducing incident rates; patrol routes and staffing are optimized.

PA & Background Music Solution

PA and background music solution with zone control and emergency broadcast.

Provides public buildings with a high-quality IP network-based public address and background music solution, addressing weak zoning control, audio degradation and disconnection from fire protection systems in traditional analog PA, and achieving integrated management of daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation.

Business Challenges

Redundant parallel systems

Background music, business PA and fire broadcast run on separate systems with duplicated equipment and high cost.

Analog audio degradation

Traditional analog PA loses quality over long distances, with unbalanced volume.

Coarse zoning

No independent volume or content control per floor or zone.

Business Impact

Emergency response risk

Fire requires manual switching to evacuation broadcast, delaying evacuation and creating a safety hazard.

Emergency delays create safety hazards

Manual broadcast switching misses the best evacuation window during fires, putting daily operations and emergency safety at risk.

Business Requirements

One integrated PA platform

One system should carry daily broadcast, background music and emergency evacuation.

Deep fire linkage

Fire signals should trigger automatic switchover to evacuation broadcast without human intervention.

Core Capabilities

Network source management

The audio server manages all program sources with remote broadcast control.

256-zone fine control

Independent volume and content per floor or zone without mutual interference.

Fire linkage switchover

Fire signals trigger evacuation broadcast at the highest priority automatically.

Schedules &amp; one-touch broadcast

Bell schedules and timed programs run automatically; emergencies broadcast with one touch.

Remote management

Control from PC and mobile anytime, anywhere.

Solution Architecture

Advantages & Delivery

Differentiated Advantages

One System, Three Uses

Covers background music, business paging and emergency evacuation in one system, replacing multiple standalone systems and cutting construction cost.

256 Independent Zones

Different zones play different content with floor-level control, meeting complex building zoning needs.

Automatic Fire Linkage

Automatically switches to evacuation broadcast upon fire alarm, no manual intervention, protecting life safety.

Remote Mobile Management

Remote control from PC and mobile; O&M staff master broadcast status anytime, anywhere.

IP Network Transmission

Runs over the existing IP network with simple cabling, flexible expansion and low cost for new zones.

Hi-Fi Sound Quality

Digital audio processing delivers clear, lossless sound for comfortable music and accurate instructions.

Implementation Process

Requirement Research

Sort out building zones, broadcast purposes and fire linkage requirements.

Solution Design

Output zoning plan, point design and equipment list.

Network Cabling

Lay transmission links over the IP network or dedicated lines.

Equipment Installation

Install speakers, audio sources and control equipment.

System Commissioning

Joint testing of zones, scheduled tasks and fire linkage.

Training & Handover

Train broadcast and fire-duty staff before handover.

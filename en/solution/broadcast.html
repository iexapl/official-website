PA & Background Music Solution

Public Address · Zoning · Emergency · BGM

For public buildings such as office towers, shopping malls, hotels, schools and hospitals, an IP network-based public address and background music solution that addresses the core pain points of weak zoning control, severe audio quality degradation and disconnection from fire protection systems in traditional PA systems, covering daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation with one system.

Solution Overview

Provides public buildings with a high-quality IP network-based public address and background music solution, addressing weak zoning control, audio degradation and disconnection from fire protection systems in traditional analog PA, and achieving integrated management of daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation.

Applicable to: Office towers, shopping malls, hotels, schools, hospitals, stations, airports and other public venues.

  • IP network-based, flexible to deploy and easy to expand
  • Digital transmission with clear audio and no degradation
  • Fine-grained control of 256 independent zones
  • Deep linkage with fire protection systems and automatic switchover in emergencies

Benefits: One system replaces multiple standalone systems, reducing construction costs; digital management improves operational efficiency; fire linkage safeguards people's lives.

Business Challenges

Operating office towers, malls, schools and hospitals, customers often struggle with fragmented PA setups, audio degradation and missing fire linkage, and urgently need an IP network PA system with fine-grained zoning and deep fire-protection integration.

Pain

Redundant parallel systems

Background music, business PA and fire broadcast run on separate systems with duplicated equipment and high cost.

Pain

Analog audio degradation

Traditional analog PA loses quality over long distances, with unbalanced volume.

Pain

Coarse zoning

No independent volume or content control per floor or zone.

Pain

Emergency response risk

Fire requires manual switching to evacuation broadcast, delaying evacuation and creating a safety hazard.

Impact

Emergency delays create safety hazards

Manual broadcast switching misses the best evacuation window during fires, putting daily operations and emergency safety at risk.

Need

One integrated PA platform

One system should carry daily broadcast, background music and emergency evacuation.

Need

Deep fire linkage

Fire signals should trigger automatic switchover to evacuation broadcast without human intervention.

Typical Scenarios: Mall background musicSchool bell schedulesFire evacuation broadcastAirport flight announcements

Target Roles: Property managers, fire duty staff, PA administrators.

Solution Architecture

PA & Background Music follows a five-layer business topology: PA Control Room, Property Office and other offices run daily applications such as Programming and Zone Paging, coordinated by the platform layer; edge devices like Amp Room and Zone Control sit on site, linking perception devices such as Sound Column and Ceiling Speaker — data flows upward and commands flow downward across the full chain.

Business layout: the user layer holds management offices (PA Control Room, Property Office…); the application layer carries daily functions; the platform layer provides PA Platform and Source Mgmt; the edge and network layer marks on-site equipment positions (Amp Room, Zone Control…); perception devices are deployed at Sound Column, Ceiling Speaker and other field locations.

PA & Background Music Solution

Public Address · Zoning · Emergency · BGM

For public buildings such as office towers, shopping malls, hotels, schools and hospitals, an IP network-based public address and background music solution that addresses the core pain points of weak zoning control, severe audio quality degradation and disconnection from fire protection systems in traditional PA systems, covering daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation with one system.

Solution Overview

Provides public buildings with a high-quality IP network-based public address and background music solution, addressing weak zoning control, audio degradation and disconnection from fire protection systems in traditional analog PA, and achieving integrated management of daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation.

Applicable to: Office towers, shopping malls, hotels, schools, hospitals, stations, airports and other public venues.

  • IP network-based, flexible to deploy and easy to expand
  • Digital transmission with clear audio and no degradation
  • Fine-grained control of 256 independent zones
  • Deep linkage with fire protection systems and automatic switchover in emergencies

Benefits: One system replaces multiple standalone systems, reducing construction costs; digital management improves operational efficiency; fire linkage safeguards people's lives.

Business Challenges

In the daily operations of large public buildings, PA systems have long suffered from fragmented construction, single-purpose design and slow emergency response.

  • Redundant construction of multiple systems: Background music, business broadcast and fire broadcast are built separately, with redundant equipment and high costs
  • Audio degradation in analog transmission: Traditional analog PA loses audio quality over long distances, with unbalanced volume
  • Coarse zoning control: Independent volume and content control by floor or zone is impossible
  • Missing fire linkage: When a fire breaks out, broadcast mode must be switched manually, delaying evacuation

Typical Scenarios: Daily background music in malls, school class bells, fire emergency evacuation broadcast, airport flight information announcements.

Intended Users: Property management staff, fire duty staff, broadcast administrators.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a four-layer network architecture of audio sources, transmission, playback and control, so that one system meets daily broadcast, background music and fire emergency evacuation needs.

  1. Audio source layer: Network audio servers, fire signal interfaces and remote broadcast control terminals
  2. Transmission layer: Carried over the existing IP network or dedicated lines, with high-fidelity digital transmission
  3. Playback layer: Zoned speaker networks supporting zone/group broadcast control
  4. Control layer: Visualized broadcast console supporting program scheduling, hierarchical control and device topology monitoring

Integration Capabilities:

  • Deep linkage with the fire alarm system: fire signals automatically trigger evacuation broadcast
  • Remote control from PC and mobile clients
  • Scheduled tasks and one-touch broadcast

Advantages & Delivery

With the IP network broadcast platform at its core, AXLTeco provides customers with a PA and background music system that is flexible to deploy, clear in audio and reliable in linkage.

Key Differentiators:

  • One system covers background music, business broadcast and emergency evacuation
  • Fine-grained control of 256 independent zones, with different content in different zones
  • Deep linkage with the fire protection system for automatic switchover to evacuation broadcast
  • Remote management and mobile control

Customer Value: Lower construction costs, higher operational efficiency, and protection of people's lives.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Solution design → Network cabling → Equipment installation → System commissioning → Training and handover.

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, remote diagnostics support, local spare parts supply.

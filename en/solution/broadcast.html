PA & Background Music Solution

Public Address · Zoning · Emergency · BGM

AXLTeco provides high-quality public address and background music solutions for commercial buildings, meeting daily operations and emergency evacuation needs.

Solution Architecture

Technical Foundation: IP network broadcast platform supporting 256 independent zones.

[Flow] Admin edits playlist → system auto-schedules by time/date/priority → speakers play corresponding content → fire signal trigger → forced override → precise evacuation broadcast → admin can remotely insert emergency notices.

[Fire Linkage] Deep integration with fire alarm systems; automatic switch to emergency evacuation broadcast upon fire trigger, precisely targeting floors to buy valuable evacuation time.

[Playback Terminals] Speaker networks deployed across building zones supporting partition/group broadcast control; different floors/areas can play different content independently.

[Transmission Layer] Audio and video streams transmitted over existing IP networks or dedicated lines, with high fidelity and simplified cabling.

[Broadcast Control Room] Located in the property office or fire control room, administrators edit programs, control zones, monitor equipment topology, and listen in real-time via visual consoles.

This solution adopts an "Audio Source-Transmission-Playback-Control" network architecture, serving daily broadcast, background music, and fire emergency evacuation.

Solution Components

Public Address System
Building-wide speaker network supporting zone and group broadcast control.
Zone Control
Independent volume and content control by floor and area without interference.
Emergency Broadcast
Linked with fire systems, automatically switching to evacuation broadcast during fires.
Background Music
Scheduled playback, playlist management and audio source control for ambience.
Remote Control
PC and mobile remote control with scheduled tasks and one-touch broadcast.
Network Broadcast
Digital IP-based broadcast system with flexible deployment and easy expansion.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to office buildings, shopping malls, hotels, schools, hospitals, stations and airports. Emergency broadcast meets national fire safety regulations; BGM creates a comfortable commercial atmosphere.

Solution Advantages

IP-based with flexible deployment; digital transmission for clear audio; fine-grained zone control supporting 256 independent zones; deep fire system linkage; remote and mobile management.

Solution Value

One system meets background music, business broadcast and emergency evacuation needs, reducing construction costs. Digital management improves efficiency and fire linkage safeguards lives.

Project Practice

Delivered PA and BGM solutions for multiple commercial complexes, hotels and schools across Lagos and other Nigerian cities, with stable operation and positive client feedback.

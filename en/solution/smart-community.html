Smart Community Solution

Smart community solution with access control, parking and security services.

For residential compounds, apartments, villas and retirement communities, an IoT and information technology based intelligent community management solution that addresses the core pain points of safety hazards, inefficient property services and inconvenient daily life, delivering a safe, convenient and comfortable living environment for residents.

Solution Overview

Provides IoT and information technology based intelligent community management solutions for residential compounds, apartments, villas and retirement communities, addressing safety hazards, inefficient property services and inconvenient daily life, and delivering a safe, convenient and comfortable living environment.

Applicable to: Residential compounds, apartments, villas, retirement communities.

  • Unified platform integrating multiple subsystems to avoid information silos
  • Face recognition for frictionless entry, improving the access experience
  • License plate recognition with frictionless payment for easier parking
  • AI video analytics proactively detect safety hazards

Benefits: Higher community safety, more efficient property services, greater resident convenience and well-being, and higher property value.

Business Challenges

In daily community management, customers often face hard-to-control access, parking disorder and slow property services, and urgently need an intelligent community system on a unified platform with frictionless access and proactive hazard detection.

Pain

Hard-to-control access

Traditional cards are easy to copy or lend; strangers slip in and visitor management is tedious.

Pain

Parking disorder

Temporary vehicles occupy spaces, monthly parking is chaotic and fee settlement is slow.

Pain

Slow property services

Slow repair response, inconvenient fee payment, low notice reach.

Pain

Elder care & safety gaps

Anomalies among seniors living alone are hard to detect and emergencies get slow response, hurting both safety and well-being.

Impact

Resident experience and safety decline

Poor access, parking and repair experiences plus unmonitored elderly residents erode property reputation and community safety.

Need

Frictionless smart access

Face-recognition entry and license-plate frictionless payment are needed.

Need

Proactive AI security

Video analytics should proactively detect falling objects and intrusions.

Typical Scenarios: Daily resident accessVisitor temporary accessVehicle entry and paymentOnline repair requestsElderly SOS calls

Target Roles: Property managers, security guards, residents, visitors.

Solution Architecture

Smart Community follows a five-layer business topology: Property Center, Committee Office and other offices run daily applications such as Surveillance and Access Control, coordinated by the platform layer; edge devices like Main Entrance and Unit Doors sit on site, linking perception devices such as Camera and Face Access — data flows upward and commands flow downward across the full chain.

Business layout: the user layer holds management offices (Property Center, Committee Office…); the application layer carries daily functions; the platform layer provides Community Hub and Video Mgmt; the edge and network layer marks on-site equipment positions (Main Entrance, Unit Doors…); perception devices are deployed at Camera, Face Access and other field locations.

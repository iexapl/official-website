Smart City Solution

Urban Governance · Public Safety · Smart Services

For city governments and operations management authorities, a city-level intelligent governance solution built on a CIM+BIM+GIS 3D digital base that addresses the core pain points of severe data silos, unclear operational status and suboptimal public resource allocation, supporting refined urban governance and sustainable development.

Solution Overview

Provides city governments and operations management authorities with city-level intelligent governance solutions built on a CIM+BIM+GIS 3D digital base, addressing severe data silos, unclear operational awareness and suboptimal public resource allocation, and supporting refined governance and sustainable development.

Applicable to: City governments, urban operations management centers, traffic management departments, municipal departments.

  • Digital twin technology maps the city between physical and virtual spaces, allowing planning schemes to be rehearsed virtually
  • The platform scales elastically, supporting phased construction from a single block to the whole city
  • Data-driven optimization of public resource allocation improves citizens' quality of life

Benefits: Higher refined governance, more efficient government services, optimized public resource allocation, and better quality of life for citizens.

Business Challenges

In urban governance, scattered and isolated data, weak awareness of city operations and decisions without data support are key bottlenecks constraining city development.

  • Severe data silos: Government affairs, transport, environment and energy data are scattered across departments and hard to share
  • Unclear operational status: No unified city operations view; problems are found by experience and handled manually
  • Suboptimal public resource allocation: Transport, energy and public service allocation lacks data support, causing supply-demand mismatches
  • High planning decision risk: Major urban construction projects lack rehearsal and verification, creating real-world risks

Typical Scenarios: Urban traffic optimization, environmental monitoring and early warning, emergency command and dispatch, public service improvement, urban planning simulation.

Intended Users: City administrators, traffic engineers, environmental staff, emergency command staff.

Solution Architecture

With the City Information Model (CIM) as the digital base, builds a three-layer architecture of urban sensing network, data platform and intelligent applications.

  1. Sensing layer: IoT sensors, video surveillance and environmental monitoring stations deployed on roads, bridges, utility networks and parks
  2. Edge layer: Edge computing nodes in districts and streets for local data preprocessing and faster response
  3. Platform layer: Aggregates multi-source data from government affairs, IoT and the internet for data governance and shared exchange
  4. Application layer: CIM+BIM+GIS 3D digital base with AI algorithms analyzing city operations and supporting scientific decision-making

Integration Capabilities:

  • Integrates data across transport, environment, municipal affairs and security
  • Digital twin technology rehearses urban planning schemes
  • Elastic scaling from a single block to the whole city

Advantages & Delivery

With digital twin technology at its core, AXLTeco provides African cities with scalable, data-driven smart city solutions mapping the physical city into virtual space.

Key Differentiators:

  • Digital twin maps the city between physical and virtual spaces; rehearsing planning schemes reduces construction risk
  • Elastic scaling supports phased construction from a single block to the whole city
  • Data-driven optimization of public resource allocation
  • Active participation in African urban digitalization, accumulating city-level project experience

Customer Value: Raises refined urban governance, promoting one-network government services and one-network unified city operations management.

Implementation Process:

Requirements research → Digital base construction → Sensing network deployment → Data platform buildout → Intelligent application development → Training and handover.

Service Assurance: Long-term operations partnership, continuous platform iteration, data security assurance.

Customer Cases

  • Outcomes: Accumulated rich city-level project implementation experience

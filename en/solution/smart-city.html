Smart City Solution

Perception · Connection · Intelligence · Collaboration

Solution Architecture

Technical Foundation: Cloud-native microservices architecture supporting elastic scaling from single blocks to entire cities.

[Flow] Front-end data collection → edge preprocessing and local decisions → central multi-source data aggregation → AI urban operation analysis → automatic alerts and recommendations → dispatch to departments → feedback loop.

[Edge Layer] Edge computing nodes in districts/streets for local data preprocessing, reducing central platform load and improving response speed.

[Perception Layer] IoT sensors, video surveillance, and environmental stations deployed on roads, bridges, pipelines, and parks; BIM models in key buildings for lifecycle management.

[City Brain Command Center] Located at the municipal government or urban operations center, managers view traffic, environment, municipal, and security status on command screens.

This solution uses City Information Modeling (CIM) as the digital foundation, building a three-layer "Perception-Data-Application" architecture for refined urban governance.

Application Scenarios

Smart City Scenarios

Applicable to urban operation monitoring, emergency command dispatch, traffic optimization, environmental monitoring, public services, and urban planning simulation. Through digital twin technology, urban plans can be rehearsed in virtual space to reduce actual construction risks and costs.

Solution Advantages

Based on digital twin technology, the solution achieves "virtual-physical mapping" of the city, enabling pre-simulation of urban planning schemes in virtual space to reduce actual construction risks. The platform has elastic scalability, supporting tiered construction from single blocks to entire cities with controllable investment and rapid results.

Solution Value

Improve the refinement level of urban governance, promote "one-network government services" and "one-network urban management." Through data-driven optimization of public resource allocation, improve citizens' quality of life and lay a digital foundation for sustainable urban development.

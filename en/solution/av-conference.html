AV Conference Solution

HD Remote Meeting · Paperless Collaboration · Smart Control

For conference rooms of enterprises, government agencies and educational institutions, an integrated audio-video conferencing solution that addresses the core pain points of low cross-regional collaboration efficiency and complex meeting equipment operation, enabling smooth HD remote communication and a paperless meeting experience.

Solution Overview

Provides enterprises, government agencies and educational institutions with an integrated audio-video conferencing solution, addressing poor cross-regional collaboration and complex meeting equipment operation, and enabling smooth HD remote meetings and a paperless office experience.

Applicable to: Enterprise conference rooms, training rooms, lecture halls, command centers, government agency meeting rooms, school multimedia classrooms.

  • HD audio-video experience with 4K video and omnidirectional audio pickup
  • Compatible with mainstream video conferencing platforms, protecting existing investments
  • Paperless meetings reduce paper consumption and improve meeting efficiency
  • One-touch meeting mode via smart central control lowers the operational barrier

Benefits: Shorter meeting preparation time, higher conference room utilization, and lower cross-regional travel costs.

Business Challenges

In the daily operations of multinational enterprises and cross-regional organizations, low meeting collaboration efficiency and complex equipment management have become prominent constraints on organizational performance.

  • High cross-regional communication costs: Branch offices are scattered and important decisions require staff travel, bringing high travel expenses and significant time loss
  • Complex meeting equipment operation: Projectors, audio, lighting and curtains must be operated separately; meeting preparation is time-consuming and difficult for non-technical staff
  • Chaotic paper-based meeting document management: Printing, distribution and recycling are cumbersome, document versions fall out of sync, and confidentiality is hard to guarantee
  • Conference room resource conflicts: Lack of unified reservation management leads to both double bookings and idle rooms

Typical Scenarios: Multinational weekly meetings, project review meetings, remote training, government work meetings.

Intended Users: Administrative staff, IT operations staff, meeting organizers, meeting participants.

Solution Architecture

Adopts a professional audio-video architecture of capture-processing-transmission-presentation, integrating local meetings, remote video conferencing and paperless meetings in three modes.

  1. Conference room capture layer: HD cameras and microphone arrays capture raw audio and video
  2. Media processing layer: Media routing servers deliver low-latency audio-video distribution; audio mixing processors ensure clear multi-party voice
  3. Paperless terminal layer: Lifting displays, electronic nameplates, screen sharing and voting
  4. Desktop access layer: PCs, tablets and phones join the meeting, supporting screen sharing and electronic whiteboards
  5. Meeting control layer: The conference management platform centrally controls devices, reservations and recording

Integration Capabilities:

  • Compatible with mainstream video conferencing platforms (Zoom, Teams, Tencent Meeting, etc.)
  • Linked with the smart central control system for one-touch control of lighting/curtains/projectors
  • Connected with office calendar systems for automated meeting reservations

Advantages & Delivery

With professional audio-video expertise and a local service network in Africa, AXLTeco provides customers with one-stop delivery from solution design to operations and maintenance support.

Key Differentiators:

  • 4K video and omnidirectional audio pickup deliver a near face-to-face meeting experience
  • The paperless meeting system automatically generates meeting minutes, reducing manual note-taking
  • One-touch smart central control lets non-technical staff complete meeting preparation in 30 seconds
  • Local technical support team in Africa for rapid fault response

Customer Value: Improved meeting efficiency, reduced travel costs, optimized conference room utilization, and greener office practices.

Implementation Process:

  1. Requirements research and conference room survey
  2. Solution design and equipment selection
  3. Installation, commissioning and system integration
  4. User training and go-live
  5. After-sales maintenance and periodic inspections

Service Assurance: One-year warranty, 7x24 hotline support, local spare parts stock.

AV Conference Solution

Integrated AV conference solution with HD remote meetings and smart control.

For conference rooms of enterprises, government agencies and educational institutions, an integrated audio-video conferencing solution that addresses the core pain points of low cross-regional collaboration efficiency and complex meeting equipment operation, enabling smooth HD remote communication and a paperless meeting experience.

Solution Overview

Provides enterprises, government agencies and educational institutions with an integrated audio-video conferencing solution, addressing poor cross-regional collaboration and complex meeting equipment operation, and enabling smooth HD remote meetings and a paperless office experience.

Applicable to: Enterprise conference rooms, training rooms, lecture halls, command centers, government agency meeting rooms, school multimedia classrooms.

  • HD audio-video experience with 4K video and omnidirectional audio pickup
  • Compatible with mainstream video conferencing platforms, protecting existing investments
  • Paperless meetings reduce paper consumption and improve meeting efficiency
  • One-touch meeting mode via smart central control lowers the operational barrier

Benefits: Shorter meeting preparation time, higher conference room utilization, and lower cross-regional travel costs.

Business Challenges

In the daily operations of multinational and multi-site organizations, customers often face high cross-regional collaboration costs, complex meeting equipment and messy paper-based materials, and urgently need an easy-to-use HD system that brings local meetings, remote collaboration and paperless work into one.

Pain

High cross-region communication cost

Branches are scattered; key decisions require travel, with high trip expenses and lost time.

Pain

Complex equipment operation

Projector, audio, lighting and curtains are operated separately; setup is slow and non-technical staff struggle.

Pain

Messy paper-based materials

Printing, distributing and collecting documents is tedious, versions fall out of sync and confidentiality is hard to ensure.

Impact

Meeting friction slows the organization

Without unified room booking, double-booking and idle rooms coexist, making meetings a hidden bottleneck for the whole organization.

Need

HD remote conferencing

4K-class video and omnidirectional pickup are needed so remote communication feels face-to-face.

Need

One-touch smart meetings

Central control should launch meeting mode in one touch so anyone can start a meeting quickly.

Typical Scenarios: Multinational weekly meetingsProject reviewsRemote trainingGovernment meetings

Target Roles: Administrative staff, IT operations staff, meeting organizers, participants.

Solution Architecture

AV Conference follows a five-layer business topology: Meeting Admin, Main Hall and other offices run daily applications such as Booking and Meeting Ctrl, coordinated by the platform layer; edge devices like Room Terminal and Venue Camera sit on site, linking perception devices such as Conf Camera and Microphone — data flows upward and commands flow downward across the full chain.

Business layout: the user layer holds management offices (Meeting Admin, Main Hall…); the application layer carries daily functions; the platform layer provides Meeting Service and Unified Comm; the edge and network layer marks on-site equipment positions (Room Terminal, Venue Camera…); perception devices are deployed at Conf Camera, Microphone and other field locations.

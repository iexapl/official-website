AI Agent Development

Custom AI agent development using large language models for enterprises.

Service Overview

AXLTeco provides end-to-end AI Agent development services, from scenario diagnosis and knowledge base construction to deployment and operations. We leverage GPT, Claude, domestic LLMs and open-source models combined with RAG, Function Calling and multi-agent orchestration to build truly deployable and measurable AI applications.

Scenario Diagnosis

Deep dive into business operations to identify high-value AI use cases and assess ROI.

RAG Knowledge Base

Build enterprise-specific knowledge bases for accurate Q&A, document parsing and knowledge traceability.

Tool Calling

Enable Agent to invoke external APIs, databases and enterprise systems via Function Calling and MCP.

Multi-Agent Collaboration

Multiple Agents working together to simulate real team workflows and handle complex tasks.

Private Deployment

Support on-premise servers and private cloud deployment, ensuring data never leaves your domain.

Service Scope

Conversational Assistant

Knowledge Q&A

City Modeling Services

City modeling with 3D reality, BIM/CIM and digital twins for smart cities.

Service Overview

AXLTeco provides end-to-end city modeling services from data collection and 3D modeling to platform integration and maintenance. We integrate multi-source data including UAV aerial survey, LiDAR, satellite remote sensing and ground collection to build high-precision, measurable, integrable and updatable 3D city models.

Multi-source Fusion

UAV oblique photography, laser point clouds, satellite remote sensing, CAD/GIS/BIM integration.

AI Modeling

AI-based 3D reconstruction, semantic segmentation and texture mapping for improved efficiency.

Lightweight Publishing

WebGL, 3DTiles, UE/Unity multi-platform lightweight publishing for smooth browser and mobile performance.

Platform Integration

Seamless integration with GIS, CIM and digital twin platforms for business visualization.

Compliance

Surveying and mapping qualifications, confidentiality compliance, strict data security standards.

Service Scope

Real-scene 3D

City-level real-scene 3D models faithfully restoring buildings, roads, vegetation and water.

Oblique Photography

UAV oblique photogrammetry for rapid high-resolution texture and 3D geometry acquisition.

Laser Point Cloud

LiDAR scanning for high-density point clouds accurately capturing complex architectural details.

BIM Modeling

Building Information Modeling with detailed component, pipeline and equipment attributes.

CIM Platform

City Information Model platform aggregating multi-source data for planning and management.

Digital Twin

City-level digital twin with real-time physical world mapping for simulation and prediction.

Service Process

1

Requirements

Define modeling scope, accuracy requirements, scenarios and delivery standards.

2

Data Collection

Collect existing CAD, GIS, BIM, imagery and plan acquisition.

3

Acquisition

UAV aerial survey, LiDAR scanning, ground supplementary photography.

4

Processing

Image correction, point cloud denoising, image matching, aerial triangulation.

5

Modeling

3D reconstruction, texture mapping, semantic annotation, LOD classification.

6

7

Delivery

8

Maintenance

Regular updates, local repairs, platform maintenance and technical support.

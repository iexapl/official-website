City Modeling Services

City modeling with 3D reality, BIM/CIM and digital twins for smart cities.

AXLTeco provides end-to-end city modeling services from data collection and 3D modeling to platform integration and maintenance. We integrate multi-source data including UAV aerial survey, LiDAR, satellite remote sensing and ground collection to build high-precision, measurable, integrable and updatable 3D city models.

服务范围

Real-scene 3D

City-level real-scene 3D models faithfully restoring buildings, roads, vegetation and water.

Oblique Photography

UAV oblique photogrammetry for rapid high-resolution texture and 3D geometry acquisition.

Laser Point Cloud

LiDAR scanning for high-density point clouds accurately capturing complex architectural details.

BIM Modeling

Building Information Modeling with detailed component, pipeline and equipment attributes.

CIM Platform

City Information Model platform aggregating multi-source data for planning and management.

Digital Twin

City-level digital twin with real-time physical world mapping for simulation and prediction.

Acquisition

Multi-rotor/fixed-wing UAVs, airborne LiDAR, terrestrial 3D laser scanners, panoramic cameras.

Software

ContextCapture, Metashape, 3ds Max, Revit, ArcGIS, SuperMap, Bentley.

Processing

Point cloud processing, AI reconstruction, texture mapping, semantic annotation, LOD, simplification.

Platform

3D engines, WebGL, Cesium, UE/Unity, cloud rendering, API/SDK development.

Qualifications

Class B surveying qualification, confidentiality certification, registered surveyors, PMP.

Standards

CH/T 9015, CityGML, 3DTiles, I3S and other domestic and international standards.

Service Process

前期阶段

Define modeling scope, accuracy requirements, scenarios and delivery standards.；Collect existing CAD, GIS, BIM, imagery and plan acquisition.；UAV aerial survey, LiDAR scanning, ground supplementary photography.；Image correction, point cloud denoising, image matching, aerial triangulation.

RequirementsData CollectionAcquisitionProcessing

后期阶段

3D reconstruction, texture mapping, semantic annotation, LOD classification.；Accuracy validation, topology check, texture quality, completeness inspection.；Deliver model data, accuracy reports and user manuals per contract.；Regular updates, local repairs, platform maintenance and technical support.

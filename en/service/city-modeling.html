City Modeling Services

City modeling services for government, urban investment, design institutes, parks and enterprises, covering real-scene 3D, BIM/CIM and digital twin.

AXLTeco City Modeling Services provide one-stop solutions from data collection, 3D modeling to platform integration and update maintenance. We integrate multi-source data including drone aerial survey, LiDAR, satellite remote sensing and ground collection to build high-precision, measurable, integrable and updatable 3D city models, providing visual decision support for smart city planning, emergency management, traffic optimization and park management.

Service Scope

Real-Scene 3D

City-level real-scene 3D models, faithfully restoring buildings, roads, vegetation, water bodies and other features.

Oblique Photography

Drone oblique photogrammetry, quickly acquiring high-resolution textures and 3D geometric information.

LiDAR Point Cloud

Laser radar scanning to obtain high-density point clouds, precisely depicting complex building and terrain details.

BIM Modeling

Building Information Modeling, finely expressing building components, pipelines and equipment attributes.

CIM Platform

City Information Modeling platform, aggregating multi-source data to support urban planning, construction and management.

Digital Twin

City-level digital twin, real-time mapping of the physical world, supporting simulation and predictive analysis.

Applications

Smart city, urban planning, emergency management, transportation, water conservancy, parks, tourism, real estate, environmental protection.

Target Clients

Government, urban investment, design institutes, parks, enterprises, system integrators.

Data Sources

Drones, LiDAR, satellite remote sensing, ground collection, CAD/GIS/BIM materials.

Technical Capabilities

Collection Equipment

Multi-rotor/fixed-wing drones, airborne LiDAR, ground 3D laser scanners, panoramic cameras.

Modeling Software

ContextCapture, Metashape, 3ds Max, Revit, ArcGIS, SuperMap, Bentley.

Processing Capability

Point cloud processing, AI reconstruction, texture mapping, semanticization, LOD classification, model simplification.

Platform Capability

3D engine, WebGL, Cesium, UE/Unity, cloud rendering, API/SDK development.

Team Qualifications

Surveying grade-B qualification, confidentiality qualification, registered surveyor, PMP certification.

Standards

CH/T 9015, CityGML, 3DTiles, I3S and other domestic and international standards.

Deliverables

3D Models

OSGB, OBJ, FBX, 3DTiles and other formats, supporting multi-platform loading.

Point Cloud Data

LAS, LAZ, E57 and other formats, preserving original measurement precision.

DOM/DEM/DSM

Digital orthophoto, digital elevation model, digital surface model.

BIM/CIM

Service Process

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Confirm modeling scope, accuracy class, use cases and delivery standards.

    DeliverableRequirements report, scope and accuracy definition Duration1–2 weeks OwnerProduct Manager
  2. 02

    Collection

    Gather existing CAD, GIS, BIM and imagery data, and prepare the acquisition plan.

    DeliverableData inventory, acquisition plan, airspace and operation permits Duration1–2 weeks OwnerSurvey Engineer
  3. 03

    Acquisition

    Carry out drone photogrammetry, LiDAR scanning and ground infill capture, and recover raw data.

    DeliverableRaw imagery, point clouds, POS data, acquisition log Duration2–4 weeks OwnerAerial Survey Crew
  4. 04

    Processing

    Perform image correction, point-cloud denoising, image matching and aerial triangulation.

    DeliverableAT results, denoised point clouds, preliminary QC record Duration1–2 weeks OwnerData Processing Engineer
  5. 05

    Modeling

    Produce the 3D reconstruction, geometric modeling and white model to obtain a measurable model.

    Deliverable3D model, white model, modeling log Duration3–6 weeks OwnerModeling Team
  6. 06

    Texturing

    Balance image color and exposure, map textures onto geometry and enhance detail for realistic appearance.

    DeliverableTextured model, texture atlas library Duration2–3 weeks OwnerModeling Team
  7. 07

    Semantics

    Classify entity semantics, assign LOD levels and lightweight the model for web loading.

    DeliverableSemantic model, LOD-tiered model, lightweight package Duration2–3 weeks OwnerModeling Team + Engineering Team
  8. 08

    Integration

    Load the model into the 3D visualization platform, expose APIs and integrate business systems.

    DeliverableDeployed platform, API documentation, integration test record Duration2–4 weeks OwnerEngineering Team
  9. 09

    Quality Check

    Verify accuracy, topology, texture quality and model completeness.

    DeliverableQC report, accuracy verification record, rectification list Duration1–2 weeks OwnerQC Team
  10. 10

    Delivery

    Deliver model data and documentation per contract, and organize customer acceptance.

    DeliverableModel data package, accuracy report, user guide, acceptance form Duration1 week OwnerProject Manager
  11. 11

    Maintenance

    Carry out periodic and partial updates, model repairs and re-surveys, and provide platform operations support.

    DeliverableUpdate releases, repair log, monthly operations report DurationOngoing (annual updates) OwnerOperations Team + Modeling Team

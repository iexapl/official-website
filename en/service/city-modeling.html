Urban Modeling Services

BIM Modeling · City Information Model · Digital Twin

Service Introduction

AXLTeco provides professional BIM modeling, urban 3D modeling and BIM+GIS integration services to empower digital twin city construction.

Service Process

01

Requirements

Clarify modeling scope, accuracy level, delivery format and timeline.

02

Data Collection

Site measurement, drawing collection, drone surveying, laser scanning.

03

Model Creation

Create BIM/CIM 3D models based on collected data, interdisciplinary collaboration.

04

Clash Detection

Run clash detection, generate conflict reports, coordinate optimization.

05

Detailed Optimization

Optimize models based on clash results, output detailed design and quantity stats.

06

Delivery & Support

Deliver final models, provide training and technical support.

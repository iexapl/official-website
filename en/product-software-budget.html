Engineering Budget Management System

Cost Estimation · Bidding · Change Orders · Settlement

The Engineering Budget Management System provides full-process cost control for construction projects, from estimation to final account settlement.

Key Features

Cost Estimation

Quick cost estimation based on BIM models or bill of quantities, supporting multiple pricing standards.

Bidding Management

Online bidding, bid evaluation and bid comparison, with full-process traceability.

Change Order Management

Online approval of design changes and visa orders, with automatic cost impact analysis.

Progress Payment

Automatic calculation of progress payment based on completed work, supporting multi-party confirmation.

Final Account Settlement

One-click generation of final account reports, with automatic reconciliation of change orders and claims.

Cost Analysis

Multi-dimensional cost analysis by project, period and category, supporting budget vs. actual comparison.

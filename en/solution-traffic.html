Smart Traffic Solution

Monitoring · Signalling · Guidance · Enforcement · Analysis

AXLTeco's smart traffic solution improves urban traffic efficiency and safety through intelligent monitoring, signal control and data analysis.

Solution Components

Traffic Monitoring
HD video coverage of major roads and intersections, with AI-based incident detection.
Signal Control
Adaptive signal control based on real-time traffic flow, reducing congestion.
Parking Guidance
Real-time parking space information and guidance to reduce traffic caused by parking searches.
Violation Enforcement
Automatic capture of red-light running, speeding and illegal parking.
Bus Priority
Bus signal priority to improve public transport efficiency.
Data Analysis
Traffic flow analysis, congestion prediction and route optimisation suggestions.

Solution Value

Applicable to urban roads, highways and transport hubs. Supports integration with existing traffic management systems.

Video Wall

Seamless · HD · Flexible · Stable

AXLTeco provides LCD and LED video wall solutions for command centres, monitoring rooms and conference rooms.

Key Features

Seamless Splicing
Ultra-narrow bezel design with splicing gap less than 1mm.
High Brightness
High-brightness panel suitable for bright environments and long viewing periods.
Long Lifespan
Industrial-grade panel with lifespan exceeding 60,000 hours.
4K Display
Support 4K Ultra HD resolution with delicate and clear images.
Flexible Combination
Support multiple splicing methods to meet diverse scenario needs.

Product Series

LCD Video Wall
Ultra-narrow bezel LCD splicing with high colour accuracy, suitable for indoor monitoring.
LED Video Wall
Seamless LED splicing with high brightness, suitable for large-scale displays.
DLP Rear Projection
High-contrast rear-projection splicing, suitable for command centres.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for monitoring centres, command centres, meeting rooms and exhibition halls. Supports multiple signal sources on screen simultaneously, enabling data visualisation and large-screen display.

System Advantages

Ultra-narrow bezels deliver near-seamless visual effects with high colour consistency. Supports 7×24 continuous operation with high reliability. Integrated with video management systems for real-time monitoring display on the wall.

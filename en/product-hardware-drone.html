Heavy-Lift Drone

High Payload · Long Endurance · Stable · Intelligent

AXLTeco provides heavy-lift industrial drones for aerial surveying, material transport and emergency rescue in engineering and infrastructure scenarios.

Key Features

High Payload
Maximum payload up to 30kg, capable of carrying surveying equipment and materials.
Long Endurance
Flight time up to 60 minutes, covering large-area operations.
RTK Positioning
Centimetre-level positioning accuracy, supporting precise surveying and mapping.
Intelligent Route Planning
One-click route generation, supporting terrain-following and waypoint flight.
Real-Time Video
HD video transmission, supporting remote real-time monitoring and command.

Product Series

FY40 Flight Platform
High-performance heavy-lift UAV platform with strong stability and expandability.
Payload Accessories
Support multiple payloads: surveying camera, LiDAR, loudspeaker, dropper, etc.
Ground Station
Portable ground control station supporting route planning, real-time monitoring and data transmission.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for aerial surveying, power line inspection, logistics transport and emergency rescue. Provide pilot training and O&M support to ensure safe and efficient drone operations.

System Advantages

All-weather operation capability with wind resistance up to Level 6. Centimetre-level RTK positioning ensures surveying accuracy. Provide full-chain services from equipment supply, training to after-sales maintenance, lowering the user threshold.

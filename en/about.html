Company Overview

AXLTeco Limited is a professional subsidiary of Shenzhen Anxinlian dedicated to overseas building intelligence and Extra-Low Voltage (ELV) system engineering. In 2024, the company formally established its overseas presence in Nigeria. Leveraging China's mature engineering management expertise and advanced technology framework, AXLTeco is committed to delivering high-quality, reliable intelligent solutions for the African market.

Primary service sectors:

  • Commercial complexes
  • Healthcare buildings
  • Industrial parks

About Us

Connecting Chinese Technology · Serving African Development

Company Profile

AXLTeco Limited (Shenzhen AXLTeco Technology Co., Ltd.) is a professional company under Shenzhen Anxinlian, specialising in overseas building intelligence and low-voltage systems engineering. Established in Nigeria in 2024, the company leverages mature engineering management experience and advanced technology from China to deliver high-quality, reliable intelligent solutions for the African market.

Our primary service sectors include: government institutions, industrial parks, commercial complexes, infrastructure, healthcare facilities and educational buildings.

Core Business

AXLTeco provides end-to-end services from consultancy, design, supply, construction, commissioning to O&M, covering 11 low-voltage systems:

  • Security & Surveillance: CCTV, Access Control, Parking Management, Intrusion Detection
  • Communication & Network: Structured Cabling, Network Communication (LAN/WAN)
  • Building Automation System (BAS), Fire Detection & Alarm System, Public Address (PA) System
  • Conference System, Digital Signage, Data Centre Infrastructure, Intelligent Building Integration System

Service Model

AXLTeco adopts a "China Technology + Local Service" dual-engine model:

China R&D Centres (Shenzhen / Chengdu)
Solution design and technology R&D, engineering detailed design and BIM modelling, remote technical support and expert consultancy, supply chain integration and equipment selection.
Nigeria Service Team
On-site project management and construction installation, localised rapid-response and after-sales service, on-site technical commissioning and system integration.

Project Experience

As a key partner of China Civil Engineering Construction Corporation (CCECC), AXLTeco has been deeply involved in multiple national-level projects in Nigeria:

  • FIRS Headquarters, Lagos: Integrated intelligent building systems for a landmark government office building
  • Mile 2 Integrated Transport Hub: Low-voltage and security systems for a complex transport node
  • Lagos Blue Line Light Rail — Phase V: Intelligent infrastructure assurance for a key rail transit project
  • Kano Railway Station: Intelligent systems for a railway station

Technical Strength

The "Lagos FIRS Smart Construction Site Management System" planned and implemented by AXLTeco won the MOHURD Science and Technology Award — First Prize, fully demonstrating the company's professional strength in building intelligence and large-scale projects.

The system monitors project progress and resource allocation in real time through digital means, enabling refined control; integrates AI recognition and sensor technology to proactively identify on-site risks and ensure construction safety; and ensures full-process data traceability so that every engineering link meets high-standard technical specifications.

Vision & Mission

Our Mission: Connecting Chinese Technology · Serving African Development. With professional engineering capabilities, we promote the development of building intelligence and contribute Chinese wisdom, Chinese standards and localised service strength to African infrastructure.

Our Vision: To become a trusted partner for building intelligence and low-voltage systems integration in Africa. Based in Nigeria, serving West Africa and the whole of Africa, working hand in hand with customers to create a smart future.

Strategic Layout: With Nigeria as the centre, gradually expanding to West African and other African markets. Continuously improving design, construction and integration capabilities to build an internationally competitive intelligent brand.

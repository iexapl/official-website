Integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering — building West Africa's intelligent future.

An integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering intelligent infrastructure provider rooted in West Africa

About Us

AXLTeco Limited is an integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering intelligent infrastructure provider serving West Africa. Its scope covers software, hardware, trade, and building intelligence and ELV systems engineering, with integrated delivery from solution design and product supply through engineering execution to operation and maintenance support. Under the brand promise “Integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering — building West Africa's intelligent future”, the company is committed to becoming West Africa's leading intelligent infrastructure partner, so that intelligent systems reliably serve every building, every scenario and every community.

Scope of Business

Software

Built to project requirements, deployed on site, integrated with existing systems.

  • Workforce, materials, safety and quality
  • IBMS and lightweight BIM
  • Dashboards and public information
  • On-site deployment and integration

Hardware

Specified and supplied to project configuration, installed and commissioned.

  • Security and access: CCTV, access control, barriers
  • Commercial display: video walls, LED, ad screens
  • Vehicle intelligence: tracking, fuel, load, dashcam
  • Energy storage, compute and special equipment

Trade

Procurement and supply of equipment and materials, batched to project schedule.

  • Sourcing to project configuration
  • Phased delivery to site sequence
  • Spare parts and replacement
  • Aligned with hardware and engineering

Building Intelligence and ELV Systems Engineering

Design, construction, commissioning and O&M by one team.

  • Video surveillance and security
  • Access control, equipment rooms, networks
  • Fire alarm, AV and broadcast
  • Smart construction, building, campus
  • Turnkey project delivery

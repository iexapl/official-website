About Us

Integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering — building West Africa's intelligent future.

An integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering intelligent infrastructure provider rooted in West Africa

About Us

AXLTeco Limited is an integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering intelligent infrastructure provider serving the West African market, covering software, hardware, trade, and building intelligence and ELV systems engineering. With software as the core, hardware as the carrier, trade as the channel, and building intelligence and ELV engineering as the delivery capability, the company provides West African clients with integrated service from solution design and product supply through engineering execution to operation and maintenance support, and aims to be the intelligent infrastructure and systems integration partner for West African scenarios.

In software, the company delivers customised software, system platforms, systems integration and digital solutions around client business scenarios, so that intelligence is genuinely embedded in buildings and operations. In hardware, it provides equipment selection, supply, integration and deployment, carrying scenario applications on reliable products. In trade, it draws on global supply chain resources to provide procurement, import and export, and supply chain coordination, connecting quality global resources with local West African demand. In building intelligence and ELV systems engineering, it provides design, construction, commissioning and O&M for structured cabling, security and surveillance, access control and building automation, network and equipment rooms, and audio-visual systems, ensuring intelligent systems land reliably and run stably.

The company focuses its service area on West Africa and respects the local market environment, languages, cultures and project delivery habits, committing to build deployable, operable and sustainable intelligent infrastructure for West African clients through localised service capability combined with global resource coordination.

Vision

To be West Africa's leading integrated software, hardware, trade and engineering intelligent infrastructure partner, so that intelligent systems reliably serve every building, every scenario and every community.

Mission

To define capability through software, carry value through hardware, connect global resources through trade, and deliver reliable systems through building intelligence and ELV engineering — building intelligent infrastructure for West African clients that is deployable, operable and sustainable.

Our Values

Six values form the company's standard of judgement for delivery in West Africa: from site selection to operation and maintenance, each corresponds to a verifiable practice.

Rooted in West Africa

We read the local market, languages and delivery habits, and take on West African projects with local teams and service capacity rather than imported templates.

Scenario-driven

System boundaries and configuration are derived from the real problems on site, confirming the scenario before fixing the solution instead of applying a generic template.

Software–hardware synergy

Software as the core and hardware as the carrier are designed, integrated and deployed in one delivery chain, so intelligence reaches the site with the equipment.

Reliable engineering

Design, construction, commissioning and O&M run through one team, with acceptance-grade engineering standards behind long-term stable operation.

Open partnership

Open interfaces and standard protocols connect existing systems and local partners; projects are delivered and sustained together with clients and suppliers.

Long-term commitment

Delivery targets sustainable operation: spares, training and O&M arrive together, so systems remain usable on real sites over time.

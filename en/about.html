About Us

From software to engineering — building systems that work in West Africa.

An intelligent infrastructure provider rooted in West Africa

About Us

AXLTeco Limited is an intelligent infrastructure provider serving the West African market, with four lines of business: software, hardware, trade, and building intelligence and ELV systems engineering. The four connect within a single project — design, equipment, construction and O&M run as one line under one team, so clients do not coordinate several suppliers.

In software, the company delivers customised software, system platforms, systems integration and digital solutions around client business scenarios, so that intelligence is genuinely embedded in buildings and operations. In hardware, it handles equipment selection and supply, delivering devices to site in the project configuration. In trade, it draws on global supply chain resources to provide procurement, import and export, and supply chain coordination, connecting quality global resources with local West African demand. In building intelligence and ELV systems engineering, it provides design, construction, commissioning and O&M for structured cabling, security and surveillance, access control and building automation, network and equipment rooms, and audio-visual systems, ensuring intelligent systems land reliably and run stably.

The company focuses its service area on West Africa and respects the local market environment, languages, cultures and project delivery habits, committing to build deployable, operable and sustainable intelligent infrastructure for West African clients through localised service capability combined with global resource coordination.

Scope of Business

Software

Built to project requirements, deployed on site, integrated with existing systems.

  • Workforce, materials, safety and quality
  • IBMS and lightweight BIM
  • Dashboards and public information

Hardware

Specified and supplied to project configuration; installation and commissioning are done by the engineering team.

  • Security and access devices: cameras, access-control terminals, barriers, turnstiles
  • Commercial display equipment: video walls, ad screens, outdoor LED
  • Vehicle terminals: tracking, fuel, load, dashcam
  • Energy storage, compute and special equipment

Trade

Procurement and supply of equipment and materials, batched to project schedule.

  • Sourcing to project configuration
  • Phased delivery to site sequence
  • Spare parts and replacement
  • Aligned with hardware and engineering

Vehicle GPS Tracker

Positioning · Tracking · Geo-Fence · Report

The vehicle GPS tracker provides real-time positioning and trajectory tracking for vehicles, improving fleet management efficiency.

Key Features

Real-Time Positioning
GPS / BeiDou / GLONASS multi-mode positioning, accuracy within 5 metres.
Trajectory Playback
Historical trajectory query and playback, supporting speed and stay analysis.
Geo-Fence
Set electronic fences, with automatic alerts for entry/exit.
Remote Cut-Off
Support remote fuel and power cut-off for anti-theft and recovery.
Driver Behaviour
Monitor speeding, fatigue driving and route deviation.
Report Analysis
Mileage, stay, speed and route optimisation reports.

System Advantages

Suitable for logistics fleets, public transport, ride-hailing and engineering vehicles. Support integration with dispatch and navigation systems.

Engineering Budget Management System

Full-lifecycle quantity management for engineering projects.

The Engineering Budget Management System is a full-lifecycle quantity management platform for engineering projects. Addressing the pain points of multi-step, low-efficiency, and high-error traditional quantity management, the system provides full-lifecycle quantity management from budget compilation and list management to over/under analysis and deviation alerts, covering budget compilation, list generation, quantity and material report output, and rebar detailing calculation. It helps enterprises precisely grasp budget execution dynamics, detect deviations timely, and improve project budget execution accuracy.

Key Features

The system covers six core modules: budget compilation, budget lists, sub-item reports, material reports, rebar detailing, and over/under analysis.

Budget Compilation

Import project branch-subitem structures and compile quantity and material budgets at leaf nodes, with version draft, publish, and iterative update based on history.

Budget List

Auto-calculate expected material consumption based on completed quantity progress, comparing with actual consumption and flagging savings or overruns.

Sub-Item Reports

Auto-aggregate expected quantity and actual consumption for each sub-item against the selected budget version baseline, generating comparison reports.

Material Reports

Aggregate material consumption from the budget list, displaying monthly actual vs. budget comparison.

Rebar Detailing

Calculate and optimise rebar cutting from construction drawings, generating rebar processing sheets and material lists.

Over/Under Analysis

Auto-aggregate expected quantity and actual consumption for each sub-item against the selected budget version baseline, generating over/under analysis reports to pinpoint budget deviation points.

System Architecture

System Architecture

The system adopts a cost full-lifecycle plus calculation-engine architecture: BIM models are extracted by the auto-quantity engine, integrated with quantity lists and cost standard libraries for estimation. An independent calculation-engine layer covers four engines: quantity calculation, quota application, multi-currency rate, and BIM auto-quantity, supporting automatic Naira/USD/RMB conversion.

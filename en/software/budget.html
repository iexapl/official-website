Engineering Budget Management System

Cost Estimation · Bidding · Change Orders · Settlement

The Engineering Budget Management System provides full-process cost control for construction projects, from estimation to final account settlement.

Key Features

Engineering Budget Quantity Preparation

Import project work breakdown structure, compile engineering quantities and material budgets at leaf nodes, supporting version draft, release, and iteration.

Engineering Budget List

Automatically calculate current expected material consumption based on completed work progress, compare with actual consumption, and highlight savings or overruns.

Sub-item Reports

Automatically aggregate expected and actual engineering quantities by selected budget version baseline, generating comparative analysis reports.

Material Reports

Summarize all material consumption in the budget list, displaying monthly actual consumption versus budget data.

Rebar Detailing

Calculate and optimize rebar cutting based on construction drawings, generating processing orders and material lists to reduce waste.

Application

System Advantages

Deep integration with BIM models enables automatic quantity extraction and cost calculation. Supports multi-project and multi-contract management, meeting the needs of general contractors and project owners.

System Architecture

The system adopts a full-lifecycle cost-management plus compute-engine architecture: BIM models are processed by the auto-quantity engine to extract engineering quantities, which are matched with the bill of quantities and cost standard library for estimation; the bidding service synchronises bidirectionally with ERP, and the change-order service links with cost analysis for dynamic cost control; the independent compute-engine layer covers four engines—quantity calculation, quota application, multi-currency exchange, and BIM auto-quantity—supporting automatic conversion among Naira, USD, and RMB; the data layer adopts a multi-database combination of MySQL+MongoDB+SQL Server, with Docker+Kubernetes elastic orchestration.

Blacklist Management System

Unified risk evaluation and blacklist platform with automatic interception.

The Blacklist System is an enterprise-wide platform that delivers unified risk-person-and-entity evaluation, blacklist management, and access interception. Addressing the difficulty of pre-identifying credit risks and safety hazards during hiring, supplier collaboration, and site access, the system uses multi-dimensional personnel evaluation, tiered blacklist management, and real-time matching engines to automatically identify and block high-risk subjects across hiring, collaboration, and access scenarios, helping enterprises build a unified risk-control system and reduce labour and supply-chain safety risks.

Key Features

The system covers three core modules: personnel evaluation, blacklist management, and access interception, building a unified risk-control line across scenarios.

Personnel Evaluation

Multi-dimensional evaluation and scoring of workers across work performance, skills, and cooperation, with attendance rates auto-calculated and a composite evaluation score generated.

Personnel Blacklist

Persons with serious violations, bad credit, or sub-standard skills are placed on the blacklist, barred from re-onboarding, and synchronised across projects.

Access Interception

Face/plate recognition terminals at gates, turnstiles, and parking areas automatically match the blacklist, intercepting high-risk persons and vehicles in milliseconds with tiered alerts linked to security response.

System Architecture

System Architecture

The system adopts a multi-source aggregation plus real-time matching architecture: manual entry, batch import, API sync, and face/plate recognition terminals converge into the list management service; Redis Cluster caches hot lists for millisecond matching; a risk-tier service determines the level and triggers SignalR real-time alerts; five scenarios — hiring, collaboration, visitors, access, and parking — embed the matching engine; RabbitMQ asynchronously generates alert logs and MongoDB stores full audit trails for compliance traceability.

HR Management System

A full-lifecycle HR digital platform for multinational construction enterprises.

The HR Management System is the core digital platform for enterprise human resource management. Addressing the complex challenges of multi-organisation structures, local compliance, and personnel deployment faced by multinational companies operating in Africa, the system integrates organisational management, labour administration, attendance, and payroll with approval workflows. It supports all official languages worldwide and West African indigenous languages such as Hausa and Yoruba, enabling full-lifecycle personnel management throughout the employee tenure and helping enterprises improve HR operational efficiency while ensuring compliance.

Key Features

The system covers six core modules: organisation structure, labour management, attendance, payroll, master data, and approval.

Organisation Structure

Unified filing and management of all on-site construction teams, covering team establishment, information maintenance, leader assignment, and entry/exit status tracking.

Labour Management

Build and maintain real-name digital profiles for project management staff and construction workers, supporting transfers, warning records, and online dismissal procedures.

Attendance

Collect attendance via facial-recognition turnstiles, access-control integration, GPS positioning, and mobile check-in. Generate individual and team attendance reports, supporting scheduling, verification, and make-up analysis.

Payroll

Auto-generate monthly payroll based on attendance and rewards/penalties, supporting payroll preparation review, holiday confirmation, and year-end bonus payout.

Master Data

Maintain master data for company types, positions, trades, and public holidays, providing standardised data for personnel classification, permission assignment, and payroll calculation.

Approval Management

Site supervisors submit worker information via mobile app, and HR managers can complete onboarding, promotion, and salary adjustment reviews with a single click.

System Architecture

Salaries are settled in the currency of each project / A single project uses only one currency

Payments and reimbursements are completed offline / The system only exports documents and reports

Application Scenarios

Suitable for multi-tier organisation structure management, cross-national labour and payroll management, intelligent attendance with device integration, and online HR approval workflows in large construction groups.

Multi-Tier Organisation Management

Multinational construction groups with subsidiaries and project sites across African countries can centrally maintain group, regional, and project organisation structures, enabling cross-tier personnel flow and precise permission assignment.

Cross-National Labour & Payroll

In mixed Chinese/foreign workforce scenarios, the system supports real-name filing, payroll calculated in each project's own currency, and Hausa/Yoruba local-language interfaces with local holiday adaptation, ensuring compliant and traceable payroll.

Advantages & Guarantee

Core Advantages

African Localization Compliance

The independent compliance engine covers African labor laws, multi-timezone attendance, and automatic local tax calculation, adapting to Naira/US Dollar/RMB salary accounting and the project-based settlement compensation model.

Full Multi-language Coverage

Supports all official languages worldwide and local ethnic languages in West Africa such as Hausa and Yoruba, enabling barrier-free use by multinational teams.

Unified Attendance Management

The attendance function supports multiple methods such as face recognition, access control linkage, and GPS positioning. Three channels of attendance data are automatically aggregated, with accurate cross-timezone conversion.

Modular Flexible Configuration

Functional modules can be flexibly configured according to enterprise scale and business characteristics, enabling rapid launch and reducing the implementation cycle.

Equipment and System Docking

Seamlessly connects with mainstream Chinese attendance and access control equipment, as well as financial systems, realizing data interconnection and sharing, and reducing manual entry.

Three-layer Technical Architecture

The server side/Web side/mobile side are layered and decoupled, easy to expand and maintain, and support continuous iteration of subsequent functions.

Delivery Services

Requirement Research

Gain in-depth understanding of the enterprise's organizational structure, compensation structure, and attendance mode, and sort out multi-timezone and multi-currency accounting rules.

System Deployment

Supports local deployment and cloud deployment, adapting to the local network environment and data compliance requirements in Africa.

Training and Launch

Provides tiered training for administrators and end users, covering organizational structure configuration, attendance management, compensation accounting, and report use.

7×24 hour remote technical support, regular system inspections and feature iterations, and the compliance engine is upgraded synchronously with updates to local regulations.

Data Security

Personnel and compensation data is stored encrypted, with hierarchical permission control and full recording of operation logs, supporting compliance audits.

Upgrade and Expansion

Supports flexible access to new attendance equipment and new tax rules, and expands management dimensions according to enterprise expansion requirements.

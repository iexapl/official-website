HR Management System

A full-lifecycle digital HR management platform covering organisational structure, labour management, attendance and payroll.

The HR Management System is the core digital platform for enterprise human resource management. Addressing the complex challenges of multi-organisation structures, local compliance and cross-border personnel deployment faced by multinational companies operating in Africa, the system integrates organisational management, labour administration, attendance and payroll with approval workflows, supporting all official languages worldwide and West African indigenous languages such as Hausa and Yoruba, to achieve full-lifecycle personnel management throughout the employee lifecycle, helping enterprises improve HR operational efficiency and ensure compliance.

Key Features

Organization Structure

Unified filing and management of all on-site construction teams, covering team establishment, information maintenance, and entry/exit status tracking.

Labor Management

Establish and maintain real-name digital profiles for management staff and construction workers, supporting transfers, warnings, and dismissal procedures.

Attendance Management

Collect daily attendance data via turnstiles, mobile apps, or devices, generating individual and team statistics with scheduling and verification.

Payroll Management

Automatically generate monthly payroll details based on attendance and penalties, supporting payroll preparation and year-end bonus distribution.

Master Data

Maintain company types, positions, trades, and holidays to provide standardized data for personnel classification and payroll calculation.

Approval Management

Enable HR managers to quickly review and approve onboarding, regularization, and salary adjustment applications submitted via the mobile app.

Application Scenarios

Multi-Level Organisation Management

Large multinational construction groups operating across Africa use the system to maintain unified group, regional and project-level organisational structures, enabling cross-level personnel flow and precise permission allocation.

Multi-Level StructurePermission Control

Cross-Border Labour & Payroll

In mixed teams of Chinese and local employees, the system supports real-name profile management, precise payroll calculation in each project's own currency within a cross-project multi-currency environment, as well as interfaces in indigenous languages such as Hausa and Yoruba and local holiday adaptation, ensuring compliant and traceable payroll calculation.

Project Currency PayrollLocal Compliance

Smart Attendance & Device Integration

Face-recognition turnstiles and GPS clock-in devices deployed on site feed attendance data into the system in real time, automatically generating individual and team attendance reports to support payroll processing.

Device IntegrationReal-Time Stats

Digital HR Approval Workflow

Foremen submit personnel transfer, regularisation and salary adjustment requests via the mobile app, while HR managers complete multi-level reviews in one click on the back end, with full audit trails improving operational efficiency.

Mobile ApprovalClosed-Loop Workflow

Modular Design

Configurable according to enterprise scale and business characteristics, enabling rapid deployment.

Multi-Language & Timezone

Supporting Chinese, English and French interfaces with multi-timezone attendance for multinational enterprises.

Device & System Integration

Seamlessly integrates with mainstream attendance devices and financial systems for data interoperability.

3-Tier Architecture

Server/Web/Mobile layered decoupling for easy scalability and maintenance.

System Architecture

The system adopts an employee full-lifecycle plus Africa-localised compliance architecture: face-recognition attendance machines, GPS devices, and access-control systems converge three attendance data streams to the attendance service, which after cross-timezone conversion flows into the payroll service; the independent compliance-engine layer covers four engines—Africa labour law, multi-currency payroll calculation, automatic tax calculation, and multi-timezone attendance—supporting Naira/USD/RMB salary disbursement and local tax compliance; seven HR services—profile, recruitment, performance, training, and organisational structure—cover the full process from onboarding to offboarding, with SQL Server archiving historical payroll records.

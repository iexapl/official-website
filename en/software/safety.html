Safety Management System

A comprehensive safety management platform for civil engineering sites, enabling risk identification, hazard rectification and emergency response closed-loop handling.

Construction sites for buildings, rail transit and municipal engineering are complex environments with high safety risks. The Safety Management System provides daily inspection, hazard recording and rectification tracking, temporary electrical management and emergency SOS response to help enterprises systematically identify and control safety risks, enforce safety accountability, reduce incident rates and safeguard personnel and equipment on site.

Key Features

Safety Inspection
Plan and execute routine or special safety inspections, supporting on-site hazard recording, photo upload, corrective assignment, and result verification.
Reward & Penalty
Record penalties for safety violations and register rewards for outstanding performance, forming a safety incentive mechanism.
Electrical Box Work Orders
Manage the full process of temporary power application, approval, implementation, and acceptance, recording voltage, current, and grounding resistance.
SOS
Provide emergency quick-assistance functionality, ensuring on-site personnel can send distress signals promptly during sudden safety incidents.

Application Scenarios

Routine Safety Inspection Closed Loop

Safety officers plan and execute routine or special safety inspections; upon discovering hazards, they upload photos, assign responsible parties and track verification results, achieving full online safety inspection management.

Hazard RectificationClosed-Loop Mgmt

Temporary Electrical Safety Control

The full process of temporary power application, approval, implementation and acceptance on construction sites is digitised, recording key parameters such as voltage, current and grounding resistance to ensure traceable electrical safety.

Power ApprovalParam Monitoring

Safety Behaviour Incentive Mechanism

Violations of safety regulations are logged with penalties, while teams and individuals with outstanding safety performance are registered for rewards, forming a safety management mechanism combining positive incentives and negative constraints.

Incentive MechanismSafety Culture

Emergency SOS Response

When on-site personnel encounter sudden safety incidents, the SOS function enables one-click distress signalling; the system automatically notifies emergency teams and records response times, enhancing on-site safety assurance.

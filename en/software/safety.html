Safety Management System

Comprehensive safety management platform for civil engineering sites, achieving risk identification, early warning, and closed-loop handling

核心功能

Risk Registry
Establish site risk source database covering high-altitude work, deep excavation, cranes, and temporary electrical systems.
Safety Inspections
Support daily patrols, special inspections, and seasonal checks with mobile photo uploads of hazards.
Hazard Management
Closed-loop management: hazard discovery → rectification notice → feedback → re-inspection acceptance.
Safety Training
Worker three-level entry education, pre-shift safety briefings, and special operation training records and assessments.
Emergency Drills
Emergency plan management, drill planning, process recording, and effectiveness evaluation.
Incident Management
Incident reporting, investigation, corrective measures, lessons learned, and knowledge沉淀.

适用场景

Construction sites, rail transit projects, municipal engineering, and industrial plant construction.

与 AXLTeco 西非工程业务的结合

Projects such as Lagos Light Rail Blue Line involve high-risk operations including deep excavation, shield tunneling, and elevated construction. The system achieves full-scenario risk control. Combined with AI cameras for real-time recognition of safety helmets, reflective clothing, and dangerous area intrusion. Compliant with Nigerian safety regulations for government inspections.

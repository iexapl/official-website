Warehouse Management System

A digital warehouse management platform covering the full process from demand submission, inbound to consumption for engineering project materials.

Engineering projects involve numerous material types with frequent turnover, and traditional manual management often leads to inventory inaccuracies, overstocking or shortages. The Warehouse Management System covers demand submission, inbound acceptance, outbound issuance, inventory checking and scrap disposal, enabling multi-warehouse coordination and material traceability to help enterprises maintain precise inventory control, reduce warehousing costs and ensure timely material supply for construction.

Key Features

Demand Management

View real-time inventory data and alerts, submit material requisitions by item or team, and track application progress with inventory analysis.

Audit Management

Perform multi-level review and e-signature on material requisitions, receiving notes, and outbound orders, ensuring authorized material flow.

Acceptance Management

Conduct quantity and quality acceptance on purchased, transferred, and centrally procured materials, supporting partial acceptance and replenishment tracking.

Inbound Management

Manage qualified material inbound registration, location allocation, and inbound transaction queries, supporting purchase receiving and returns.

Outbound Management

Process material outbound picking, location allocation, and outbound transaction recording based on requisitions, supporting returns and cancellations.

Inventory Check

Perform periodic or ad-hoc warehouse inventory checks, supporting barcode scanning, batch operations, and surplus/shortage adjustments.

Scrap Materials

Register, check, and track scrap materials, monitoring disposal status and quantity changes.

Team Consumption

Track material issuance and consumption by construction team, analyzing material usage costs and efficiency per team.

Earthwork Management

Manage earthwork faces and vehicle profiles, track transport routes, and daily/monthly statistics of loading, filling, and dumping volumes.

Basic Maintenance

Maintain material lists, category definitions, supplier database, location information, and approval workflow configurations.

Application Scenarios

Multi-Warehouse Inventory Control

Group headquarters, regional and project warehouses are managed under a unified multi-level inventory system, providing real-time visibility into global stock status and alerts, enabling demand-driven allocation and precise inventory control.

Multi-Warehouse CoordinationInventory Alerts

Material Acceptance & Inbound Tracking

Incoming materials undergo quantity and quality acceptance; qualified items are registered for inbound storage with location allocation, supporting partial acceptance and replenishment tracking to ensure accurate and traceable inbound records.

Acceptance & InboundLocation Mgmt

Team Requisition & Consumption Analysis

Construction teams submit material requisitions based on needs; after approval, outbound picking and transaction logging are completed, while the system monthly tracks each team's material consumption to support cost accounting and efficiency analysis.

Team ConsumptionCost Analysis

Inventory Check & Scrap Management

Warehouse staff scan and check materials by shelf location, supporting batch operations and surplus/shortage adjustments. Scrap materials are registered after counting, generating disposal records with full lifecycle tracking to ensure inventory accuracy.

Scan to CheckInventory Accuracy

System Advantages

Supports multi-warehouse and multi-owner management, adaptable to warehouses of different sizes and business models. Open API interfaces for integration with ERP, transport management and e-commerce platforms.

System Architecture

The system adopts a full-process warehousing plus operation-engine architecture: PDA scanners, RFID readers, vehicle terminals, scales, and printers form five types of field-operation terminals; the independent warehouse-operation engine layer covers four engines—inbound, outbound, internal, and wave picking—driving the full process from receiving→QC→putaway and order aggregation→picking-path optimisation; the barcode/RFID service collects operation data in real time, the inventory service achieves high-concurrency deduction based on Redis Cluster, and the transfer service guarantees distributed transactional consistency based on DotNetCore.CAP; the multi-warehouse management service uniformly controls group headquarters warehouses, regional warehouses, and project warehouses.

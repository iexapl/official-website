IBMS

Unifies all intelligent building subsystems onto a single platform for centralised monitoring, coordinated control and smart operation.

Modern buildings contain numerous intelligent subsystems including building automation, security, fire protection and energy management. Operating independently leads to fragmented management and delayed response. The Intelligent Building Management System (IBMS) integrates multiple subsystems, provides centralised monitoring, intelligent coordination and energy optimisation to achieve unified equipment management and collaborative control, helping enterprises improve operational efficiency, reduce energy costs and ensure safe building operation.

Key Features

System Integration

Integrate 20+ building subsystems including BA, security, fire, access control, parking and energy through multi-protocol gateways such as BACnet, Modbus and OPC UA, enabling centralised management of cross-brand devices on a single open platform.

Unified Monitoring

Display full building equipment status, alarms and energy data on one unified map, with automatic anomaly pop-ups and precise positioning, delivering visualised central monitoring through digital-twin technology fusing BIM and IoT data.

Intelligent Analysis

AI-powered anomaly detection, energy consumption prediction and fault diagnosis based on real-time operational data, proactively identifying risks and generating actionable maintenance recommendations.

Automated Control

Scenario-based automated control across subsystems, such as work mode, energy-saving mode and emergency mode, with fire alarms automatically triggering access release, broadcast switching, elevator homing and smoke extraction.

Energy Management

Real-time monitoring and analysis of water, electricity and gas consumption through time-series databases, combined with AI algorithms to generate energy-saving optimisation strategies for green building operations.

Report & Decision Support

Automatic generation of comprehensive operation reports covering equipment efficiency, energy trends and fault rates, providing data-driven support for management decisions.

System Advantages

Applicable to commercial complexes, office buildings, hotels, hospitals and other large buildings. Supports local deployment and cloud deployment.

System Architecture

The system adopts a multi-protocol adaptation plus digital-twin architecture: the five subsystems BA/FA/SA/CA/OA are uniformly accessed through multi-protocol gateways Modbus/BACnet/OPC UA/MQTT; the linkage-control service implements distributed transactions based on DotNetCore.CAP, with fire alarms automatically triggering access release, broadcast switching, elevator homing, and smoke-exhaust startup; the digital-twin service fuses BIM+IoT data to achieve 3D visualisation through the WebGL engine; the energy-analysis service delivers real-time water-electricity-gas monitoring and AI energy-saving strategies based on a time-series database, and the predictive-maintenance service generates maintenance recommendations based on equipment operating data.

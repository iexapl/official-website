Information Disclosure System

Unified platform for internal notices, policies, and project announcements.

The Information Disclosure System provides unified publishing, tiered management, and convenient query for internal notices, policies, and project announcements. Addressing the pain points of scattered information channels and difficulty in unified management and precise reach, the system supports multi-level category classification, information publishing and announcement management, reading statistics, and multi-terminal display, achieving centralised publishing and convenient retrieval. It helps enterprises improve information transparency and ensure critical updates reach employees in time.

Key Features

The system covers six core modules: information publishing, announcement management, multi-level category, query, reading statistics, and multi-terminal display.

Information Publishing

Rich-text editing, multimedia attachments, and category tagging, with one-click publishing to PC portal, APP, displays, and other terminals after review.

Announcement Management

Full lifecycle management: drafting, approval, publishing, and retraction.

Multi-Level Category

Flexible multi-level category and tiered permissions based on organisation structure.

Query

Full-text search and multi-dimensional advanced filtering.

Reading Statistics

Real-time tracking of readers, reading duration, and coverage, with IM push reminders for unread persons.

Multi-Terminal Display

Distributed broadcast-control architecture pushes content to PC portal, mobile APP, lobby displays, and touch screens.

System Architecture

System Architecture

The system adopts a multimedia orchestration plus distributed broadcast-control architecture: six content sources — text, image, video, web, business data, and weather API — are aggregated into a single library. An independent broadcast-control engine covers four engines: programme scheduling, timed dispatch, emergency insertion, and zoning distribution. Emergency insertion uses SignalR to override all terminals at the highest priority.

Advantages & Guarantee

Core Advantages

Multimedia Content Orchestration

Supports unified orchestration of six types of content sources: text, images, videos, web pages, business data, and weather API, enabling rich and diverse information presentation.

Distributed Playback and Control Architecture

Based on the distributed playback and control architecture, content is synchronously pushed to multiple terminals with consistent display effects, enabling centralized and efficient management.

Emergency Insertion Capability

The emergency insertion service covers the current playback of all terminals with one click at the highest priority, ensuring emergency notifications are delivered instantly.

IM Tool Integration

Supports integration with IM tools such as WeChat Work, DingTalk, and Feishu, making information reach more timely and the notification closed loop more complete.

Government Affairs Disclosure Compliance

Supports government affairs disclosure scenarios, meets the compliance requirements of government information disclosure, and the column structure can be flexibly configured.

Scheduled Scheduling

Supports scheduled release, automatic removal, and periodic cycle of programs, automating content operation and reducing manual intervention.

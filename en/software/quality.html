Quality Management System

A full-lifecycle quality management platform for civil engineering projects, enabling process inspection, issue closed-loop handling and quality traceability.

In building construction, municipal roads, rail transit and other civil engineering projects, construction quality is directly linked to structural safety and service life. The Quality Management System enables process inspection, first-piece acceptance, on-site supervision and reward and penalty tracking to help enterprises identify and rectify quality issues promptly, ensuring every process meets technical standards and driving continuous quality improvement.

Key Features

Quality Inspection
Conduct process quality inspections, record findings, and track corrective actions to ensure construction meets quality standards and technical specifications.
Reward & Penalty
Record quality-related penalties and rewards for meeting standards, driving continuous quality improvement and accountability.
First-piece Management
Perform special acceptance and archiving of the first sample before mass construction, establishing it as the quality reference for subsequent work.
Supervision Duty
Implement on-site supervision for critical processes and high-risk operations, recording construction conditions, issues found, and corrective measures.

Application Scenarios

Construction Process Quality Inspection

On building and municipal road projects, quality inspectors carry out on-site process inspections, record findings and assign responsible parties for rectification, achieving a full online closed loop from issue discovery to correction and verification.

Process InspectionIssue Rectification

First-Piece Sample Acceptance

Before mass construction, the first sample component undergoes special acceptance; once approved, it serves as the quality reference standard for subsequent work, ensuring consistency across large-scale operations.

First-Piece AcceptanceQuality Benchmark

On-Site Supervision of Critical Processes

For high-risk critical processes such as deep excavation, shield tunneling and elevated construction, supervisors implement on-site duty supervision, recording construction conditions and corrective measures in real time to ensure controlled operations.

On-Site SupervisionHigh-Risk Control

Quality Reward & Penalty Traceability

Quality defects during construction are logged with accountable penalties, while teams meeting standards receive rewards, forming a quality incentive and constraint mechanism that drives continuous improvement.

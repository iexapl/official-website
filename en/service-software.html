Custom Software Development

Tailored · End-to-End Service · Multi-Platform

AXLTeco provides professional enterprise management system and government system development services, covering the full lifecycle from requirements analysis, solution design, development implementation, testing and deployment to O&M support.

Service Scope

Enterprise Management Software
Custom development of ERP, CRM, OA, project management and other enterprise management systems.
Government Systems
Government management platforms, approval systems and public information systems for government institutions.
Industry-Specific Software
Professional software solutions tailored for construction, transport, energy and other industries.
Multi-Platform
Unified development for Web, mobile and mini-program to meet diverse usage scenarios.

Development Process

Requirements Analysis → Solution Design → UI/UX Design → Development → Testing → Deployment → O&M Support
Each stage is overseen by senior engineers to ensure on-time, high-quality delivery. We adopt agile development with iterative delivery, allowing clients to see results and provide feedback throughout the process.

Technical Capabilities

Our R&D team has extensive experience in large-scale system development, proficient in modern architectures including front-end/back-end separation, microservices and cloud-native deployment, selecting the most suitable technology stack for each project.

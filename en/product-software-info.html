Information Display System

Content Management · Multi-Screen · Scheduled Playback · Remote Control

The Information Display System centrally manages digital display content for buildings, campuses and public spaces.

Key Features

Content Management

Support text, images, videos, web pages and other media formats, with playlist editing.

Multi-Screen Management

Unified management of multiple display screens, supporting zone-based content distribution.

Scheduled Playback

Set playback schedules by time, date and priority for automated operation.

Remote Control

Remote screen on/off, volume adjustment, screenshot and status monitoring.

Emergency Insertion

One-click insertion of emergency notifications, overriding current playback.

Data Display

Real-time display of business data, weather, news and other dynamic information.

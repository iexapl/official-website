Advertising Screen

HD · Bright · Durable · Remote

AXLTeco provides indoor and outdoor advertising display screens for commercial complexes, stations and public spaces.

Key Features

HD Display
1080P/4K HD resolution with vivid colours and wide viewing angles.
Remote Control
Support remote content publishing, power on/off and brightness adjustment.
Multimedia Playback
Support video, image, text and web page multiple content formats.
Scheduled Publishing
Support scheduled playback plans with automatic content switching.
Split Screen
Support multi-screen split display for versatile usage.

Product Series

Wall-Mounted Screen
Slim design, suitable for elevators, corridors and shops.
Floor-Standing Kiosk
Floor-standing design, suitable for lobbies, shopping malls and airports.
Outdoor High-Brightness
High-brightness design clearly visible in sunlight, waterproof and dustproof.
Touch Interactive Screen
Support multi-touch, suitable for information query and self-service.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for commercial streets, transport hubs, government halls, hospitals and schools. Supports centralised control and group management, enabling multi-screen linkage and unified information publishing.

The centralised control platform supports unified content creation, group publishing and playback monitoring. Rich content templates and broadcasting strategies lower the operational threshold. Equipment supports 7×24 stable operation with convenient after-sales maintenance.

Smart Community Solution

Access · Parking · CCTV · Property · Services

AXLTeco smart community solution uses IoT and information technology to provide residents with safe, convenient and comfortable community living.

System Architecture

The system adopts a cloud-edge-device three-layer architecture. The cloud hosts the community management platform for data aggregation and analysis; the edge layer deploys local servers and gateways for real-time processing; the device layer includes access control, parking, CCTV and sensors.

Solution Components

Smart Access
Face recognition, card, PIN and remote entry with visitor QR code passes.
Smart Parking
ANPR, parking guidance, cashless payment and automatic monthly renewal.
Video Surveillance
Full community coverage with高空抛物 detection and face recognition.
Smart Property
Online repair requests, fee payment, notices and complaint handling.
Convenience Services
Parcel lockers, EV charging, smart waste sorting and community marketplace.
Elderly Care
Abnormal behaviour monitoring for seniors, SOS emergency calls and health records.

Application Architecture

The application architecture uses a community middleware platform integrating security, access, property and lifestyle service groups. The resident app provides access, parking, repairs and services; the property backend manages devices and work orders.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to residential communities, apartments, villas and care homes. Provides safe and convenient access, efficient property services and rich lifestyle services.

Solution Advantages

Unified platform integrating multiple subsystems; face recognition for seamless access; ANPR cashless parking; AI video analytics for proactive safety; online property services improving efficiency and transparency.

Solution Value

Improve community safety and property service efficiency, enhance resident convenience and satisfaction, and increase property values. Data-driven operations reduce costs and create better living experiences.

Project Practice

Delivered smart community solutions for multiple residential projects covering access control, parking, CCTV and property services, significantly improving management efficiency and resident satisfaction.

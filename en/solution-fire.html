Fire Alarm Solution

Smoke · Heat · Control Panel · Linkage

AXLTeco fire alarm solution provides early fire detection and intelligent linkage control to maximise personnel safety and minimise property loss.

System Architecture

The system consists of four subsystems: fire detection, alarm control, linkage execution and fire management. Detection includes smoke, heat, flame and gas detectors; alarm control handles signal processing and zone broadcasting; linkage controls sprinklers, smoke exhaust and emergency lighting.

Solution Components

Fire Detectors
Photoelectric smoke, heat, flame and combustible gas detectors.
Alarm Control Panel
Intelligent fire alarm control panel supporting multi-loop, high-capacity device connections.
Manual Call Point
Manual fire alarm buttons for immediate activation when fire is spotted.
Sounder & Beacon
Fire sounders and beacons for zoned or building-wide evacuation broadcasts.
Linkage Control
Automatic linkage with sprinklers, smoke exhaust, fire shutters, emergency lighting and access release.
Graphic Display
Fire control room graphic display unit showing fire locations at a glance.

Application Architecture

The application architecture adopts distributed design with detectors and modules connected via loop buses to the alarm panel. The panel interfaces with graphic display units, fire linkage controllers and city fire remote monitoring centres.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to commercial buildings, residential complexes, industrial plants, warehouses, hospitals and schools. The system meets national fire safety regulations and holds fire product certifications.

Solution Advantages

High-sensitivity detectors for early warning; intelligent algorithms reducing false alarms; powerful linkage control executing fire plans automatically; network monitoring connecting to city fire platforms; graphical display for intuitive fire location.

Solution Value

Detect fire and issue alarms at the earliest moment, buying precious time for evacuation. Automatically link firefighting equipment to control fire spread. Complete event logging and remote monitoring ensure compliance and reduce casualties and losses.

Project Practice

Provided fire alarm system design, installation and commissioning for multiple commercial complexes, industrial plants and residential communities, all passing fire inspections with stable operation.

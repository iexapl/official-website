AI Agent Development

Cutting-Edge Tech · Business Automation · Intelligent Decision-Making

AXLTeco leverages large language model (LLM) technology to provide intelligent AI Agent development services for enterprises, empowering business automation and intelligent decision-making.

Key Features

Natural Language Understanding
Based on large language models, understanding complex business instructions without preset fixed workflows.
Multi-Modal Perception
Supporting text, voice, image and other input methods for comprehensive business environment perception.
Tool Invocation & Integration
Invoking internal enterprise systems and external APIs to complete complex task closed-loops.
Knowledge Base Retrieval
Answering professional questions based on enterprise knowledge bases with continuous learning and optimisation.
Autonomous Decision-Making
Possessing a closed-loop "understand–reason–act" capability to autonomously complete business processes.
24/7 Operation
Automating repetitive work around the clock, freeing up human resources.

Application Scenarios

Customer Service Agent
Automatically respond to customer enquiries, handle common issues and intelligently escalate to human agents.
Document Processing Agent
Automatically read, summarise and classify documents, extracting key information to generate reports.
Data Analysis Agent
Connect to business databases and automatically generate analysis reports and decision recommendations.
Process Automation Agent
Take over repetitive approval, data entry and notification workflows, freeing up human resources.

System Advantages

We provide end-to-end services from business diagnosis and scenario design to deployment and O&M. Combined with AXLTeco's deep expertise in building intelligence, we are particularly adept at AI application implementation in vertical domains such as engineering management, security monitoring and equipment O&M. Compared with general-purpose AI Agents, our solutions are deeply optimised for construction and engineering scenarios, offering higher accuracy and lower deployment costs.

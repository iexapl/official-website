Video Wall

Ultra-Narrow Bezel · 4K · Multi-Screen · 24/7 Operation

AXLTeco provides 46–55-inch ultra-narrow-bezel LCD video walls with bezels as narrow as 0.88mm, 4K resolution and 24/7 continuous operation, paired with professional image processors for multi-window splitting, cross-screen roaming and flexible source switching — ideal for command centers, meeting rooms, exhibition halls and security monitoring.

Ultra-Narrow Bezel

Bezel 0.88mm–3.5mm selectable, near-seamless visuals even at close viewing distance.

4K UHD Display

3840×2160 resolution, 178° wide viewing angle, accurate color, comfortable for long viewing.

Flexible Wall Layouts

2×2, 3×3, 4×4 and more splicing modes, landscape and portrait freely combined.

24/7 Operation

Industrial-grade panels with ≥60,000-hour life for non-stop display.

Multi-Source Input

HDMI, DVI, VGA, DP and SDI inputs with window splitting and cross-screen roaming.

Smart Color Calibration

Automatic white-balance and brightness leveling keep wall-wide color deviation under 3%.

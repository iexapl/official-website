Pedestrian Turnstile

Tripod · Swing · Flap Gate · Face Recognition · Counting

AXLTeco provides tripod turnstiles, swing gates, flap gates and full-height turnstiles supporting face, fingerprint, IC card and QR verification, linkable with attendance and access systems for precise personnel control and real-time counting in metro stations, offices, factories and scenic spots with high foot traffic.

Fast Face-Verified Passage

Links with access terminals for 0.3s face verification, eliminating buddy punching.

High-Throughput Design

Flap gates pass 30–40 persons/min, swing gates 25–35 persons/min for peak flows.

Anti-Tailgating & Anti-Reverse

Infrared detection triggers alarms and closes the lanes, blocking unauthorized tailgating.

Multiple Verification

Face/fingerprint/IC card/QR/NFC, with combined verification for higher security.

Real-Time Counting

Bi-directional entry counting uploaded to the management platform in real time, exportable reports.

Anti-Pinch & Anti-Collision

Flap gates rebound on obstruction, swing arms can be pushed open, lanes open automatically on power failure.

Vehicle Fuel Sensor

High-Precision Fuel Monitoring · Real-Time Data · Abnormal Alert · Analysis

AXLTeco provides capacitive and ultrasonic vehicle fuel-level sensors with ±1% accuracy, supporting real-time fuel data acquisition, theft/leak alerts and mileage-fuel correlation analysis — for refined fuel cost control of logistics fleets, construction machinery and mining vehicles.

±1% Accuracy

Capacitive and ultrasonic technologies, ±1% full-range accuracy, 0.1mm resolution.

4G Real-Time Transmission

Fuel data uploaded to the cloud per second, remote monitoring and history queries.

Anomaly Alerts

Real-time SMS/app alerts for fuel theft, leakage and abnormal consumption.

Mileage-Fuel Analytics

Automatically correlates mileage with fuel, generating per-100km reports for dispatch optimization.

Anti-Tamper Design

Tamper detection, encrypted transmission and intelligent anomaly recognition.

Local Data Cache

Stores ≥7 days of data offline; automatic re-upload once the network recovers.

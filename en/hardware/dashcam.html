Vehicle Dashcam

H6-4G dashcam with real-time video, AI analytics and GPS for fleet management.

The H6-4G Vehicle Dashcam integrates 4G real-time video transmission, AI intelligent analysis and Beidou/GPS dual-mode positioning. With a 2MP sensor supporting 1080P HD recording, H.265 compression, infrared night vision and smart white light, it enables real-time tracking, live video, driving behavior analysis and automatic emergency event reporting.

4G Real-time

LTE Cat.1 communication for real-time video upload, trajectory playback and remote monitoring.

AI Analytics

Human detection, abnormal behavior recognition, automatic alerts during parking monitoring.

Dual-mode Positioning

GPS + Beidou dual-mode, 64 channels, <2m accuracy, AGPS support.

HD Night Vision

Infrared night vision + smart white light for clear images day and night.

Large Storage

TF card up to 512GB, H.265 compression saves storage space.

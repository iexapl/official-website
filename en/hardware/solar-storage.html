Solar & Storage

Solar+Storage+Inverter · Peak Shaving · Self-Consumption · Grid Export

AXLTeco provides integrated PV + storage + inverter solutions for C&I and residential scenarios, intelligently managing generation, storage dispatch, load supply and grid feed-in to raise renewable self-consumption and cut electricity costs.

High-Efficiency PV Modules

Monocrystalline PERC/TOPCon modules with ≥22% efficiency and excellent low-light performance.

LiFePO4 Storage

Safe, long-life batteries with ≥95% charge/discharge efficiency and deep cycling support.

Hybrid Inverter

PV + storage + grid triple input, MPPT efficiency ≥99.5%, on/off-grid operation.

Smart Energy Management

AI-optimized generation/storage/consumption strategies raise self-consumption above 80%.

Excess to Grid

Surplus power feeds into the grid automatically for feed-in revenue and better ROI.

Remote Monitoring & O&M

App/web monitoring of generation, storage and consumption with remote fault diagnosis.

Solar PV Modules

Six power classes from 600 W to 625 W, module efficiency 22.21%–23.14%, N-type TOPCon bifacial dual-glass.

A module you can verify, not just specify. This series uses N-type TOPCon cells in a dual-glass bifacial structure, offered in six power classes from 600 W to 625 W with module efficiency from 22.21% to 23.14%. A 2.0 mm anti-reflection coated front glass paired with a 2.0 mm heat-strengthened back glass, in a 2382 × 1134 × 30 mm frame at 32.4 kg, suits both commercial and residential roofs sized by available area and orientation. This page covers the module layer only; inverter, storage and energy management belong to the system layer and are covered under Interfaces & Compatibility.

N-type TOPCon

N-type cells built on Tunnel Oxide Passivating Contacts (TOPCon) show lower LID and LeTID degradation and better low-light performance.

HOT 2.0 Technology

N-type modules with HOT 2.0 technology offer better reliability and efficiency.

SMBB Technology

Better light trapping and current collection improve module power output and reliability.

Anti-PID Guarantee

Minimises the chance of degradation caused by PID phenomena through optimisation of cell production technology and material control.

Dual-sided Power Generation

Dual-sided gain increases with rear-side light exposure at a bifaciality of 80±5%, significantly reducing LCOE.

Dual-glass Construction

Encapsulated between a 2.0 mm anti-reflection front glass and a 2.0 mm heat-strengthened back glass, with an anodised aluminium alloy frame.

Model Selection

One module format spans six power classes. Apart from power and current, dimensions, weight, glass and frame are identical across classes, which simplifies mixed-class layouts and spares within a single project. Select the class by project capacity and grid conditions.

600 W class

  • The main grid-tied class for C&I and residential rooftops
  • shares one format and spares set with the 625 W class

605 W class

  • The adjacent class for fine-tuning capacity to available area
  • interchangeable with the 600 W class in mixed layouts

610 W class

  • The middle class for mid-size rooftop projects
  • mixable with 605 W and 615 W within one string

615 W class

  • The usual class when output per area and cost must balance
  • select by working back from the grid-connection cap

620 W class

  • The preferred class for high-irradiance, well-oriented rooftops
  • only 5 W below the 625 W class

625 W class

  • The top class of this format
  • projects constrained by area take their maximum installed capacity here

Advantages & Guarantee

Core Advantages

Six classes, one format

The six classes from 600 W to 625 W share the 2382 × 1134 × 30 mm format and 32.4 kg weight, simplifying mixed-class layouts and spares.

High efficiency band

Module efficiency from 22.21% to 23.14% delivers more installed capacity on the same roof area.

Bifacial dual-glass

2.0 mm glass on both faces with a heat-strengthened rear, bifaciality of 80±5%, with gains from rear-side light.

Low degradation

1% in the first year and 0.4% each year thereafter, keeping output at no less than 87.4% of the initial value at the end of year 30.

Mechanical load & certifications

Certified for 5400 Pa front and 2400 Pa rear static loads; IEC61215/61730 (2016), IEC61701/62716/60068/62804, ISO9001/14001/45001.

System-level delivery

Beyond modules, we also deliver inverters, storage and energy management, with parameters confirmed per project.

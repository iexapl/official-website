Solar PV Modules

Six power classes from 600 W to 625 W, module efficiency 22.21%–23.14%, N-type TOPCon bifacial dual-glass.

View specificationsRequest a sizing recommendation

A module you can verify, not just specify

This series uses N-type TOPCon cells in a dual-glass bifacial structure, offered in six power classes from 600 W to 625 W with module efficiency from 22.21% to 23.14%. A 2.0 mm anti-reflection front glass paired with a 2.0 mm heat-strengthened back glass makes it configurable for both C&I and residential rooftops by available area and orientation. This page covers the module layer in full; storage, inverters and energy management belong to the system layer and are described under System Composition.

  • Six power classes, sized to the project
  • Dual-glass bifacial, gains from rear irradiance
  • Every figure traceable, none estimated

Core Technology

N-type TOPCon

N-type cells built on Tunnel Oxide Passivating Contacts (TOPCon) show lower LID and LeTID degradation and better low-light performance.

HOT 2.0 Technology

N-type modules with HOT 2.0 technology offer better reliability and efficiency.

SMBB Technology

Better light trapping and current collection improve module power output and reliability.

Anti-PID Guarantee

Minimises the chance of degradation caused by PID phenomena through optimisation of cell production technology and material control.

Dual-sided Power Generation

Dual-sided gain increases with rear-side light exposure at a bifaciality of 80±5%, significantly reducing LCOE.

Dual-glass Construction

Encapsulated between a 2.0 mm anti-reflection front glass and a 2.0 mm heat-strengthened back glass, with an anodised aluminium alloy frame.

Models and Specifications

One module format spans six power classes. Apart from power and current, dimensions, weight, glass and frame are identical across classes, which simplifies mixed-class layouts and spares within a single project.

System Composition

A grid-tied solar and storage system is built from four layers: modules, inverter, storage and energy management. Module-layer specifications are in the tables above; the inverter, storage and energy management layers are system-level configurations whose parameters are confirmed per project and are not part of the module datasheet.

PV modules

Sized by available roof area and orientation across six power classes; see the specification tables above.

Grid-tie inverter

DC-to-AC conversion and grid-tie control, capacity matched to array size and grid conditions.

Storage unit

Covers self-consumption and backup duration, capacity sized to the load profile.

Energy management

Monitors generation, storage and consumption, dispatching charge and discharge by policy.

Delivery and Service

Delivery

  • Site survey: Assess irradiance, usable installation area and grid connection conditions, then deliver module layout and capacity recommendations.
  • Sizing and design: Select power class, inverter and storage configuration against project scale and load profile, and issue the equipment schedule.
  • Installation and commissioning: Mounting structures, modules, inverters and storage, including system-level testing.
  • Grid connection and training: Support permitting and acceptance testing, and train operators on monitoring, policy adjustment and alarm handling.

Service

  • Module warranty: 12-year product warranty and 30-year linear power warranty, per the formal warranty document.
  • Local response: Service teams in Nigeria and other regions providing technical support, spare parts and on-site maintenance.
  • Remote O&M: Real-time monitoring of generation and storage, remote fault diagnosis and proactive alerts, with 7×24 technical support.

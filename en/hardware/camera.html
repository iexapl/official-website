视频监控系统

HD IP Camera · NVR Recorder · VMS Platform · AI Analytics

AXLTeco 提供从高清网络摄像机、网络录像机（NVR）到Video Management Platform的完整视频监控系统，覆盖前端采集、智能分析、存储回放与集ZH管理全链路，适用于Construction工地、Office楼宇、交通Hub、工业园区等Scenario的安防需求。

Full-Color HD Acquisition

2MP to 8MP resolution, H.265+ encoding, low-light full-color imaging, up to 120dB WDR.

AI Intelligent Analytics

Face recognition, behavior analysis, line crossing detection, people counting — deep learning algorithms for precise false alarm filtering.

Flexible Storage Solutions

4 to 128-channel NVR options, RAID support with intelligent retrieval, H.265+ saves up to 75% storage.

Remote Mobile Management

Mobile app real-time preview and playback, instant alarm push, monitor on the go.

Harsh Environment Adaptation

IP67 protection, anti-corrosion coating, -30°C to +60°C wide temperature operation.

Scalable Deployment

Scale from single building to city-level networks, ONVIF/GB/T 28181 compatible with mainstream brands.

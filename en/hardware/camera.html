Video Surveillance Equipment

AXLTeco delivers a complete video surveillance ecosystem — from HD network cameras and NVRs to video management platforms — covering the full chain of front-end capture, intelligent analytics, storage and playback, and centralized management for construction sites, office buildings, transport hubs, and industrial parks.

Full-Color HD Capture

2 MP to 8 MP resolution, H.265+ encoding, low-light full-color imaging, and up to 120 dB wide dynamic range.

AI Intelligent Analytics

Face recognition, behavior analysis, line-crossing detection, and people counting, with deep-learning algorithms that filter false alarms accurately.

Flexible Storage Options

4 to 128-channel NVRs with RAID support and smart retrieval; H.265+ saves up to 75% storage space.

Remote Mobile Control

Live view and playback from a mobile app with instant alarm push, keeping you connected to every site.

Harsh-Environment Ready

IP67-rated with anti-corrosion coating and -30°C to +60°C wide-temperature operation for demanding outdoor use.

Scalable Deployment

Grow from a single building to a city-scale network in phases, with ONVIF / GB/T 28181 compatibility across major brands.

Model Selection

Video Surveillance Equipment covers multiple categories and models to meet diverse scenario and budget requirements.

Network Bullet / Turret Cameras

  • 2 MP–8 MP options
  • Fixed or motorized lens
  • Up to 80 m IR night vision

Network Dome / Mini Dome

  • Smart retrieval and backup

Interfaces & Compatibility

Video Surveillance Equipment supports rich physical interfaces and network protocols, compatible with mainstream platforms and third-party systems.

Physical Interfaces

  • RJ45 10/100M auto-sensing Ethernet
  • RS-485 / RS-232 serial ports
  • Audio in/out (RCA)
  • Alarm in/out (dry contact)
  • microSD slot (up to 512 GB)
  • HDMI / VGA video output (NVR)
  • USB 2.0 / 3.0 ports

Network Protocols

  • TCP/IP, IPv4/IPv6, HTTP/HTTPS
  • ONVIF (Profile S/G/T)
  • RTSP, RTP, RTCP
  • GB/T 28181 national standard
  • ISAPI, SDK
  • SMTP, FTP, NTP, DDNS
  • SNMP, UPnP, PPPoE

Compatible Platforms

  • Hik-Connect mobile app
  • iVMS-4200 client
  • HikCentral unified platform
  • DSS Ultimate — Dahua video integration platform
  • DSS Professional — Dahua video integration platform (Professional edition)
  • DSS OneBox — Dahua video integration platform
  • DSS Express — Dahua video integration platform
  • DSS7116D / DSS7116DR — Dahua video integration platform models
  • DMSS — Dahua mobile app
  • Third-party VMS platforms (ONVIF)
  • Plugin-free browsing in major browsers

Third-Party Compatibility

  • Works with Hikvision, Dahua, and other major IPC brands
  • Third-party ONVIF camera onboarding
  • SDK for secondary development
  • Open ISAPI interface

Access Control & Attendance

Access Control & Attendance Equipment covers multiple categories and mode...

AXLTeco provides multimodal time-and-attendance and access control terminals covering face, fingerprint, IC card and PIN verification, from desktop check-in devices to outdoor waterproof all-in-one units, meeting the entry management needs of enterprises, construction sites, schools and residential communities, with automated attendance statistics and intelligent access security.

0.3s Face Recognition

Visible-light/NIR dual-lens liveness detection, 1:N recognition rate >99.5%, stable in low-light environments.

Accurate Dry/Wet Fingerprint Reading

Semiconductor fingerprint sensor with self-learning algorithm, >98% recognition for dry and wet fingers, 5,000 template capacity.

Multi-Card Compatibility

IC, ID, CPU cards and NFC phones, with anti-passback and normally-open/normally-closed door linkage.

Attendance Reports

Automatic shift scheduling, late/early-leave statistics and overtime calculation, export to Excel/PDF, HR system integration.

Hierarchical Access Permissions

Multi-door interlock, anti-passback, first-card opening and multi-card opening with 256-level fine-grained permissions.

Offline Standalone Operation

Attendance and door access continue during network outages; data is stored locally and synced automatically once reconnected.

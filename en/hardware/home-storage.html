Residential Storage

Residential Energy Storage covers multiple categories and models to meet dive...

AXLTeco provides 5–25kWh modular residential energy storage systems using LiFePO4 batteries, supporting PV input, grid charging, emergency backup and smart app management — for green power and outage protection in homes, villas and small shops.

5–25kWh Flexible Expansion

5kWh per module, up to 5 modules in parallel to 25kWh — expand on demand.

PV-Storage-Charging Integration

Links with PV inverters and EV chargers for self-consumption, storage and EV charging.

Emergency Backup

0ms switchover to battery on outage, keeping lights, fridge and network running.

Smart App Management

Real-time generation, consumption and battery status with remote mode control.

Peak-Valley Savings

Charge at valley, discharge at peak to cut household electricity bills.

Auto On/Off-Grid Switching

Smart grid detection automatically toggles grid-tied/islanded mode without manual action.

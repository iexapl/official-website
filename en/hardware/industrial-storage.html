C&I Energy Storage

LFP Battery · 215kWh Cabinet · Peak-Valley Arbitrage · Demand Mgmt

AXLTeco provides 215kWh standard C&I energy storage cabinets using LiFePO4 batteries and liquid cooling, supporting peak-valley arbitrage, demand management, emergency backup and on/off-grid switching — for power cost optimization and supply reliability in factories, malls, data centers and charging stations.

215kWh Standard Cabinet

215kWh per cabinet, parallel expansion to MWh scale, modular plug-and-play.

Peak-Valley Arbitrage

Charge at valley, discharge at peak to cut electricity bills; 3–5 year payback.

On/Off-Grid Switching

0ms switchover to island mode on grid failure, keeping critical loads powered.

Liquid Cooling

Smart liquid thermal control keeps cell temperature spread <3°C, extending life to 15 years.

Demand Management

Real-time load monitoring and intelligent charge/discharge control to avoid demand penalties.

Cloud Smart Dispatch

AI-optimized charge/discharge strategies with price and load forecasting for maximum revenue.

Heavy-Lift Drone

High Payload · Long Endurance · Stable · Intelligent

AXLTeco provides 100kg-class heavy-lift cargo drones with 100kg max payload and 10km range, supporting FPV piloting and automatic waypoint flight — for material delivery in remote areas, emergency rescue, agriculture and power-line inspection.

100kg Payload

150kg MTOW with 100kg effective payload for large-item transport.

10km Range

10km per flight, with multi-drone relay and automatic battery swapping to extend coverage.

All-Weather Flight

IP55 protection, -20°C to +50°C, operational in light rain and breeze.

FPV + Auto Waypoints

Dual modes: first-person real-time control and automatic route flight — simple and safe.

Fast Battery Swap

Smart battery bay swaps in 3 minutes for uninterrupted operations.

Emergency Jettison

Auto payload jettison and return in emergencies to protect the aircraft.

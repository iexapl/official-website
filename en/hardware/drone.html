Heavy-lift Drone

Three product lines — VTOL fixed-wing survey and security UAVs, heavy-lift cargo eVTOLs and multi-rotor passenger eVTOLs — covering payloads from the hundred-kilogram class up to 800 kg, combining vertical take-off site flexibility with fixed-wing cruise efficiency for surveying, security patrol, logistics, urban air mobility and emergency response.

The range is built on two configurations. The compound-wing UAV combines a four-rotor lift system with a flying-wing fixed wing in one airframe, pairing vertical take-off with fixed-wing cruise efficiency for survey and patrol missions where the launch site is constrained but the operating radius is long.

The tandem-rotor cargo platform places two rotors fore and aft; counter-rotation cancels torque and removes the tail rotor, giving high payload transport capacity in the tonne-class take-off weight bracket for point-to-point movement of construction and emergency supplies.

All three lines share one design logic: full-scenario modular payloads, in-house flight control and an in-house ground station, so mission payloads are swapped by scenario rather than locked to an airframe.

Model Selection

The platform is organised into categories by mission profile and ground support model; the four categories map to different configurations, payloads and continuity requirements.

UAV Platforms

VTOL fixed-wing survey & security UAV

  • Vertical take-off plus fixed-wing cruise, balancing constrained launch sites with long-range operations
  • Terrain survey, ortho and oblique photography, perimeter and pipeline security patrol
  • Select by payload: three-axis stabilised gimbal (visible, infrared and laser ranging) for security; ortho full-frame or five-lens camera for survey. For high assurance, choose triple-redundant flight control with dual-antenna differential heading

Heavy-lift cargo eVTOL

  • Tandem-rotor configuration, high payload transport in the tonne-class bracket
  • Point-to-point movement of construction and emergency supplies, island and mountain resupply
  • Select by single-trip payload and operating radius: 500 kg and 800 kg classes. Consider airframe size against the launch site, and site provisioning for both fast charge and battery swap

Multi-rotor passenger eVTOL

  • Multi-rotor passenger platform, hybrid range extension for endurance
  • Urban air mobility, short intercity transport, sightseeing, emergency medical transfer
  • Select by seat count and flight time; confirm airworthiness and airspace compliance for the operating location first — this page does not determine certification routes on the customer behalf

Energy replenishment & ground support

  • Fast charge and battery swap, with ground station and flight control support
  • Continuous operations, uncrewed support points, multi-aircraft rotation
  • Select by daily sorties: battery swap with a battery inventory reserved for dense schedules; fast charge to reduce battery spend on lighter schedules

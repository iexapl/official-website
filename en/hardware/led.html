Outdoor LED Display

Bright · Waterproof · Stable · Remote

AXLTeco provides a full range of P2.5–P10 outdoor full-color LED displays with 5,000–10,000 nits brightness and IP65 protection, supporting front maintenance and quick modular replacement — for outdoor advertising, stadiums, stage rental and traffic guidance.

Ultra-High Brightness

5,000–10,000 nits, clearly visible under midday sun with automatic brightness adjustment.

IP65 Waterproof & Dustproof

Die-cast aluminum cabinet, fully sealed — stable in rainstorms and dust.

Modular Front Maintenance

Modules, power supplies and receiver cards accessible from the front; 3-minute replacement.

Seamless Unlimited Splicing

Standard 500×500mm / 500×1000mm cabinets tile to any size with seamless images.

Remote Cluster Management

Cloud platform manages multiple screens: content publishing, status monitoring, fault alerts.

High Refresh, Deep Gray

3,840Hz refresh, 16-bit grayscale — no scan lines on camera, crisp live footage.

Categories & Models

The product line covers P2.5–P10 pixel pitches in fixed-installation and rental series for different viewing distances and applications.

Fine-Pitch Fixed Screen P2.5–P4

  • 2.5–4mm pixel pitch
  • Clear at close range
  • Building walls/plazas
  • 3,840Hz refresh

Standard Fixed Screen P5–P6

  • 5–6mm pixel pitch
  • Mid-distance viewing
  • Best value
  • Billboards/stadiums

Coarse-Pitch Fixed Screen P8–P10

  • 8–10mm pixel pitch
  • Long-distance viewing
  • Ultra-high brightness
  • Highways/rooftops

Rental Series

  • 500×500mm cabinet
  • Quick assembly
  • Flight-case transport
  • Stage/events/exhibitions

Digital Signage

HD Digital Signage · Remote Publishing · Multimedia · Split Screen

AXLTeco provides wall-mounted, floor-standing and outdoor high-brightness advertising screens from 32 to 86 inches, supporting 4K resolution, remote content publishing, scheduled playback and smart split-screen for information display and advertising in malls, hotels, restaurants, airports and stations.

4K HD Display

3840×2160 resolution, IPS panel with 178° viewing angle, vivid color and fine detail.

Outdoor High Brightness

Outdoor models reach 2,500–3,000 nits, clearly readable in direct sunlight with auto light sensing.

Remote Cloud Management

Publish content from web/app, schedule power on/off, monitor device status in real time.

Smart Split-Screen

1/2/3/4-zone layouts mixing video, images, text and web pages.

IP65 Outdoor Protection

Outdoor models: IP65 waterproof and dustproof, -20°C to +50°C, UV resistant.

Scheduled Playback

Switch content automatically by time slot, date and scenario — unattended operation.

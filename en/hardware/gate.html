Vehicle Barrier Gate

License Plate Recognition · Fast Barrier · Multi-Control · Smart Parking

AXLTeco provides straight-boom, fence, advertising barrier gates and LPR all-in-one units, with adjustable boom speed of 0.6–6 seconds and multiple triggers — license plate recognition, remote control, ground loop and radar — for vehicle entrance management in parking lots, communities, factories and toll stations.

High-Accuracy LPR

AI deep-learning algorithm with >99.5% recognition, supporting NEV plates and special plate formats.

0.6s Fast Boom

High-speed servo motor, stepless 0.6–6s boom travel, tripling traffic throughput.

Multi-Layer Anti-Collision

Pressure-wave, ground-loop, radar and infrared anti-crushing — four layers of protection for people and vehicles.

Remote Cloud Management

App-based remote gate opening, temporary parking billing, space reservation and reports.

All-Weather Operation

IP54 protection, -30°C to +60°C, LED fill light for clear night recognition.

Multiple Boom Types

Straight, fence, advertising and folding booms, customizable 2–6 m length.

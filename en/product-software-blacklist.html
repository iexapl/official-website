Blacklist System

Entry · Verification · Alert · Analysis

The Blacklist System enables unified management of person and vehicle blacklists, enhancing security prevention capabilities.

Key Features

Blacklist Entry

Support manual entry, batch import and automatic entry by rule.

Multi-Source Verification

Real-time verification against access control, parking and video surveillance systems.

Instant Alert

Automatic alert upon blacklist hit, supporting SMS, APP and on-site sound-light alarm.

Access Record

Full record of blacklist person/vehicle access attempts for traceability and evidence.

Risk Analysis

Analysis of blacklist hit trends, high-frequency locations and time patterns.

Whitelist Management

Support whitelist configuration to avoid false positives.

Application Scenarios

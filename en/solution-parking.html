Parking Management Solution

LPR · Guidance · Car Finder · Automated Payment

System Architecture

Intelligent Parking System Architecture

AXLTeco parking solution adopts a three-tier "Edge-Gateway-Cloud" architecture: the edge layer deploys license plate recognition cameras, parking space detectors, and barrier controllers; the gateway layer aggregates local data and enables real-time decisions; the cloud platform provides unified management, data analytics, and remote O&M. The system supports both on-premise and hybrid cloud deployment, flexibly adapting to parking facilities of all sizes.

Solution Components

License Plate Recognition
HD cameras with >99% recognition rate, supporting multiple plate formats.
Parking Guidance
Ultrasonic/video detectors monitor space status in real time, with guidance screens directing drivers to available spots.
Reverse Vehicle Finding
Drivers enter their plate number to locate their vehicle and get navigation to the parking spot.
Unattended Payment
Automatic billing, QR code payment, and contactless payment eliminate the need for manned exits.

Application Architecture

The parking management platform uses a microservices architecture with core services including vehicle recognition (multi-format plates), parking space management (real-time status sync), billing & settlement (flexible rate configuration), and reporting & analytics (multi-dimensional operations analysis). The front-end provides three entry points: admin portal, driver mini-program, and attendant app, with data synchronized in real time via message queues to ensure stability under high concurrency.

Application Scenarios

Commercial Complexes

Multi-level parking management for shopping malls and office clusters, supporting peak-hour tidal lane scheduling.

Residential Communities

Automatic entry for residents, visitor pre-registration, flexible monthly/temporary parking billing.

Transportation Hubs

Long-term parking management and quick vehicle locating for airports and railway stations.

On-Street Parking

Smart retrofitting of urban roadside parking spaces with dual-mode detection and electronic payment.

Solution Advantages

The system supports both cloud management and local deployment, flexibly configurable according to parking facility scale. Integration with building management and fire systems enables intelligent parking as part of a unified smart building ecosystem.

Solution Value

Increase parking turnover by over 30%, reduce labor costs by 60%, and shorten average vehicle search time to under 2 minutes. Data-driven optimization of parking resources creates continuous value for operators.

Project Practice

AXLTeco has deployed intelligent parking systems in multiple commercial complexes and residential communities in Lagos, achieving unattended toll collection and remote centralized management with stable operation exceeding 24 months.

Outdoor LED Display

Bright · Waterproof · Stable · Remote

AXLTeco provides outdoor LED display screens for stadiums, squares, building facades and transport hubs.

Key Features

High Brightness
Brightness up to 8000 nits, clearly visible in sunlight.
Weatherproof
IP65 protection rating, waterproof, dustproof and corrosion-resistant.
Remote Control
Support remote content update, brightness adjustment and status monitoring.
Multimedia Playback
Support video, image, animation, live streaming and other formats.
Energy Saving
Intelligent brightness adjustment with low-power design reduces operating costs.

Product Series

Fixed LED Display
Permanently installed on building facades or columns, suitable for outdoor advertising.
Rental LED Display
Modular design with quick assembly/disassembly, suitable for events and performances.
Transparent LED
High transparency rate without affecting building lighting, suitable for glass curtain walls.
Creative LED Display
Support creative shapes such as curved, cylindrical and spherical forms.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for outdoor advertising, sports venues, traffic guidance and stage performances. Supports large-size seamless display with stunning visuals and strong eye-catching effect.

System Advantages

IP65 protection rating ensures long-term stable outdoor operation. Automatic brightness adjustment adapts to day/night environmental changes, saving energy. Deep integration with broadcasting systems supports scheduled playback and remote management.

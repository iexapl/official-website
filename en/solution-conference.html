AV Conference Solution

Conference · Video · Paperless

AXLTeco provides high-quality audio-visual conferencing solutions for enterprises, supporting local meetings, remote video conferencing and paperless meetings.

System Architecture

The system adopts a four-layer architecture: audio-video capture, signal processing, network transmission and terminal display. The capture layer includes HD cameras and microphone arrays; the processing layer handles encoding, decoding and mixing; the transmission layer delivers data over IP networks; the display layer covers large screens and projectors.

Solution Components

Local Conference System
Professional audio, conference microphones, mixing console, amplifiers and speakers.
Remote Video Conferencing
HD video terminals, cameras and microphone arrays supporting multi-site joining.
Paperless Meeting
Motorised display terminals, electronic nameplates, screen sharing, voting and auto-generated minutes.
Room Booking
Online room reservation, attendance tracking and usage analytics.
Recording & Streaming
Full meeting recording, live streaming and on-demand playback.
Smart Control
One-touch control of lighting, curtains, projection and audio equipment.

Application Architecture

The application architecture supports local room control, remote collaboration platforms and unified meeting management. The local control system manages lighting, curtains and AV equipment; the remote platform supports multi-site HD video conferencing and screen sharing.

Application Scenarios

Applicable to corporate meeting rooms, training rooms, auditoriums and command centres. Supports local meetings, remote conferencing, training broadcasts and paperless office scenarios.

Solution Advantages

HD audio-video with 4K video and omnidirectional pickup; compatible with mainstream video platforms; paperless system improves efficiency and reduces waste; smart control simplifies operation; comprehensive meeting management optimises room utilisation.

Solution Value

Improve meeting efficiency, reduce travel costs and support cross-regional team collaboration. Paperless practices green concepts and intelligent management optimises resource allocation.

Project Practice

Deployed AV conferencing systems for multiple enterprises, government agencies and educational institutions, from small meeting rooms to large auditoriums, providing one-stop design and installation services.

AV Conference Solution

HD Video · Clear Audio · Intelligent · Collaborative

AXLTeco provides audio-visual conference system solutions for enterprise meeting rooms, training rooms and command centres.

Solution Components

HD Video Conference
4K camera and large screen for clear and smooth remote video communication.
Professional Audio
Professional sound reinforcement, microphone array and acoustic treatment for clear sound quality.
Intelligent Control
One-key scene switching, automated control of lights, curtains and equipment.
Wireless Collaboration
Wireless screen sharing, supporting multi-device simultaneous projection.
Recording & Live Streaming
Meeting recording, live streaming and on-demand for knowledge retention.
Room Booking
Online room booking, occupancy display and usage statistics.

Supports integration with mainstream video conferencing platforms such as Zoom, Teams and Tencent Meeting. Supports local deployment and cloud hybrid deployment.

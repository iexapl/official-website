HR Management System

Employee Records · Attendance & Access Control Equipment · Payroll · Recruitment

The HR Management System is the core digital tool for enterprise human resource management, helping organisations achieve full-lifecycle personnel management from recruitment to offboarding.

Key Features

Employee Records

Centralised management of employee information, contracts and certificates, supporting batch import and intelligent search.

Attendance & Access Control Equipment

Multiple attendance methods (clock-in, facial recognition, GPS), with automatic report generation.

Payroll

Flexible payroll configuration, automatic salary/social insurance/tax calculation, multi-currency support.

Performance

Customisable appraisal templates supporting KPI/OKR/360° evaluation, linked to payroll.

Recruitment

Full-process management from job posting, CV screening, interview scheduling to offer approval.

Training

Training plan creation, course management, learning record tracking and effectiveness evaluation.

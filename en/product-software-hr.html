HR Management System

Employee Records · Attendance · Payroll · Recruitment

The HR Management System is the core digital tool for enterprise human resource management, helping organisations achieve full-lifecycle personnel management from recruitment to offboarding.

Key Features

Employee Records Management
Centralised management of employee basic information, contracts and qualification certificates, supporting batch import and intelligent search.
Attendance Management
Support multiple attendance methods (clock-in, facial recognition, GPS positioning), with automatic attendance report generation.
Payroll Management
Flexible payroll structure configuration, automatic calculation of salary, social insurance and tax, supporting multi-currency disbursement.
Performance Management
Customisable performance appraisal templates, supporting KPI / OKR / 360-degree evaluation, with performance results linked to payroll.
Recruitment Management
Full-process management from job posting, CV screening, interview scheduling to offer approval.
Training Management
Training plan creation, course management, learning record tracking and training effectiveness evaluation.

System Advantages

The system adopts a modular design, configurable according to enterprise scale and business characteristics. Supports multi-organisation, multi-language and multi-time-zone, particularly suitable for multinational enterprises' African branches. Seamlessly integrates with mainstream attendance devices and financial systems in China for data interoperability.

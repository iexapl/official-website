IBMS

Integration · Monitoring · Analysis · Optimisation

The Intelligent Building Management System (IBMS) integrates multiple building subsystems onto a unified platform, enabling centralised monitoring, intelligent analysis and optimised operation.

Key Features

System Integration
Integrate CCTV, access control, fire alarm, HVAC, lighting, elevator and other subsystems.
Unified Monitoring
One-map display of building equipment status, alarm information and energy consumption data.
Intelligent Analysis
AI-powered anomaly detection, energy consumption prediction and fault diagnosis.
Automated Control
Scenario-based automated control, such as "work mode", "energy-saving mode" and "emergency mode".
Energy Management
Real-time monitoring and analysis of energy consumption, with optimisation suggestions.
Report & Decision Support
Automatic generation of operation reports to support management decision-making.

Application Scenarios

Multi-Subsystem Unified Integration
Unified platform management of 20+ subsystems including BA, security, fire, access control, parking and energy.
Smart Building O&M
Real-time monitoring of building equipment operation status, with automatic anomaly alerts and coordinated response.
Energy Optimisation Management
Real-time monitoring of water, electricity and gas consumption, analysing usage patterns and generating energy-saving recommendations.
Equipment Predictive Maintenance
Predicting equipment failures based on operational data, scheduling maintenance in advance and extending equipment lifespan.

System Advantages

Applicable to commercial complexes, office buildings, hotels, hospitals and other large buildings. Supports local deployment and cloud deployment.

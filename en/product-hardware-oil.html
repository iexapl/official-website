Vehicle Fuel Theft Sensor

Real-Time · Precise · Alert · Traceable

The vehicle fuel theft sensor monitors fuel level changes in real time, effectively preventing fuel theft and leakage.

Key Features

Liquid Level Monitoring
Real-time monitoring of fuel tank level changes with millimetre-level accuracy.
Vibration Detection
Detect abnormal vibrations and tampering behaviour, triggering timely alarms.
GPS Linkage
Linked with vehicle positioning system to accurately locate vehicles with abnormal events.
Abnormal Alarm
Automatic alarm when abnormal fuel drop or illegal tank cap opening is detected.
Data Reporting
Real-time reporting of monitoring data to the management platform, supporting historical queries.

Product Series

Vehicle Liquid Level Sensor
High-precision ultrasonic/capacitive liquid level sensor, suitable for various fuel tanks.
Vibration Sensor
High-sensitivity vibration detection, capable of identifying tampering, drilling and other abnormal behaviours.
Smart Terminal
Vehicle terminal integrating liquid level, vibration and temperature multi-dimensional monitoring.
Monitoring Platform
Centralised management of all sensor data, supporting report generation and abnormal analysis.

Application Scenarios

Suitable for engineering fleets, logistics transport and tanker monitoring. Effectively prevents vehicle fuel theft, reduces operating costs and safeguards fleet assets.

System Advantages

Multi-dimensional monitoring with low false-alarm rate and high recognition accuracy. Deep integration with fleet management systems achieves a closed loop from monitoring to handling. Supports local African network environments, with data stored locally or synchronised to the cloud.

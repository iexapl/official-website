Modélisation Urbaine Services

BIM Modelling · City Information Modelling · Digital Twin

AXLTeco provides professional BIM modelling, urban stereo modelling and BIM+GIS integration services, empowering digital twin city construction.

Services de Modélisation BIM

Bâtiment Information Modelling (BIM) is the core technology of building digital transformation. We provide:
• BIM Model Creation: Create high-precision 3D building information models based on design drawings
• Detailed Design: MEP coordination, structural node optimisation, construction simulation
• Clash Detection: Automatically identify conflicts between discipline models to eliminate construction risks in advance
• Quantity Surveying: Automatically generate material lists and cost estimates based on the model

Modélisation d'Information Urbaine (CIM)

Ville Information Modelling (CIM) extends BIM technology from individual buildings to the city scale. Combining oblique photography, LiDAR point cloud scanning and other technologies, we construct city-level 3D real-scene models to support full-lifecycle applications in urban planning, construction and management.

Intégration BIM + GIS

Deep integration of Building Information Modelling and Geographic Information Systems enables seamless connection between "micro-level buildings" and "macro-level cities". Application scenarios include:
• Ville Intelligente Foundation: Provide a unified 3D spatial data foundation for various smart city business systems
• Emergency Command: Disaster simulation, evacuation analysis and emergency resource dispatch visualisation
• Infrastructure Management: 3D digital management of underground utilities, transport facilities and energy infrastructure
• Full-Lifecycle Project Management: Full-process digital control from planning and design to construction and O&M

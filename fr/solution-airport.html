Aéroport Intelligent Solution

Sécurité · Operations · Passenger · Baggage · Energy

AXLTeco's smart airport solution integrates multiple intelligent systems to improve airport security, operational efficiency and passenger experience.

Solution Components

Sécurité Check
Intelligent security screening, facial recognition boarding and perimeter intrusion detection.
Vidéosurveillance
Full coverage of terminals, aprons and cargo areas, with AI-based anomaly detection.
Passenger Flow Analysis
Real-time passenger flow monitoring and prediction to optimise security check and boarding arrangements.
Baggage Tracking
RFID-based full-process baggage tracking to reduce loss and misdelivery.
Gestion Énergétique
Intelligent control of lighting, HVAC and elevators in terminals for energy saving.
Flight Display
Real-time flight information display and intelligent voice broadcasting.

Solution Value

Applicable to civil airports, general aviation airports and airport logistics parks. Compliant with civil aviation industry security standards.

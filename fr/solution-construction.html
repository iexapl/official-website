Intelligent Construction Site Solution

Safety · Quality · Progress · Environment · Personnel

AXLTeco's smart construction site solution uses IoT, AI and BIM technologies to achieve digital management of personnel, equipment, materials, environment and safety.

Solution Components

Personnel Management
Real-name registration, access control attendance and labour real-name management.
Safety Surveillance
AI-based identification of non-compliant behaviour such as missing safety helmets and unauthorised area intrusion.
Tower Crane Surveillance
Anti-collision, overload and wind speed monitoring for safe tower crane operation.
Surveillance Environnementale
Real-time monitoring of PM2.5, PM10, noise and temperature/humidity, with automatic spray control.
Vidéosurveillance
Full-site HD video coverage, supporting remote real-time viewing and playback.
Progress Management
BIM-based 4D progress simulation and deviation analysis for refined control.

Solution Value

Help construction enterprises improve safety management, control project progress and reduce management costs. The solution has been successfully applied to the Lagos FIRS project and won the MOHURD Science and Technology Award — First Prize.

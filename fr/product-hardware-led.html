Outdoor LED Display

Bright · Waterproof · Stable · Remote

AXLTeco provides outdoor LED display screens for stadiums, squares, building facades and transport hubs.

Caractéristiques Clés

High Brightness
Brightness up to 8000 nits, clearly visible under strong sunlight.
Waterproof & Dustproof
IP65 protection, adaptable to rain, dust and high temperatures.
Énergie Saving
Low-power LED chips and intelligent brightness adjustment to reduce energy consumption.
Front & Rear Maintenance
Support front and rear maintenance for easy repair and replacement.
Remote Surveillance
Real-time monitoring of screen status, temperature and fault alerts via the cloud.
Multiple Installation
Support wall-mounted, pole-mounted, hoisting and ground-embedded installation.

System Advantages

Provide full services from steel structure design, foundation construction, screen installation to commissioning. Support customised pixel pitch and size.

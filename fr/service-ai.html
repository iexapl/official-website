AI Agent Development

Cutting-Edge Tech · Business Automation · Intelligent Decision-Making

AXLTeco leverages large language model (LLM) technology to provide intelligent AI Agent development services for enterprises, empowering business automation and intelligent decision-making.

Qu'est-ce qu'un Agent IA ?

An AI Agent is an intelligent system capable of autonomously perceiving its environment, making decisions and executing tasks. Unlike traditional software, Agents IA possess a closed-loop "understand–reason–act" capability, handling complex business processes like a human assistant.
Propulsé par a large language model (LLM) as the "brain", combined with external tool invocation, knowledge base retrieval and automated execution, Agents IA can:
• Understand natural language instructions without preset fixed workflows
• Invoke internal enterprise systems and external APIs to complete complex tasks
• Answer professional questions based on knowledge bases and continuously learn and optimise
• Automate repetitive work 24/7

Scénarios d'Application

Customer Service Agent
Automatically respond to customer enquiries, handle common issues and intelligently escalate to human agents.
Document Processing Agent
Automatically read, summarise and classify documents, extracting key information to generate reports.
Data Analysis Agent
Connect to business databases and automatically generate analysis reports and decision recommendations.
Process Automation Agent
Take over repetitive approval, data entry and notification workflows, freeing up human resources.

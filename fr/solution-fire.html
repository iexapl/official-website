Fire Detection & Alarm Solution

Smoke · Heat · Control Panel · Linkage

AXLTeco's fire detection and alarm solution provides early fire detection and intelligent linkage control to maximise life safety and minimise property loss.

Solution Components

Fire Detectors
Photoelectric smoke, heat, flame and combustible gas detectors.
Détection Incendie Control Panel
Intelligent fire alarm control panel supporting multi-loop, large-capacity device connection.
Manual Call Point
Manual fire alarm buttons for immediate alarm triggering by personnel.
Sounder & Beacon
Fire sounders and beacons for zoned or building-wide evacuation broadcast.
Linkage Control
Automatic linkage with sprinklers, smoke exhaust, fire shutters, emergency lighting and access release.
Graphic Display
Fire control room graphic display unit for clear visibility of fire location.

Solution Value

The system complies with national fire protection standards and is certified for fire products. Supports networked supervision, with alarm information uploadable to city fire remote monitoring centres.

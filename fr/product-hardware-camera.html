Vidéosurveillance

HD · Intelligent · Durable · Scalable

AXLTeco provides high-definition video surveillance solutions, covering cameras, NVRs, storage and display devices to meet security needs in diverse scenarios.

Caractéristiques Clés

HD Cameras
2MP to 8MP resolution, supporting H.265 encoding, low-light full-colour and WDR.
Intelligent Analysis
Built-in facial recognition, behaviour analysis, perimeter intrusion detection and people counting.
NVR & Storage
Support 16 to 128 channels, RAID storage, with intelligent retrieval and backup.
Mobile Surveillance
Real-time viewing and playback via mobile APP, with alarm push notifications.
Environmental Adaptation
IP67 protection, anti-corrosion coating, suitable for outdoor and harsh environments.
Scalable Deployment
Support phased expansion from a single building to a city-wide network.

System Advantages

Compatible with mainstream camera brands, supporting ONVIF and GB/T 28181 protocols. Provide end-to-end services from design, supply, installation to commissioning.

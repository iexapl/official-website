Campus Intelligent Solution

Sécurité · Attendance · Access · Energy · Information

AXLTeco's smart campus solution builds a safe, efficient and intelligent campus environment for schools and universities through the integration of multiple systems.

Solution Components

Campus Security
Perimeter intrusion detection, video surveillance and emergency alarm for all-round campus safety.
Intelligent Attendance
Facial recognition attendance for students and staff, with automatic statistics and parent notifications.
Contrôle d'Accès
Dormitory, library and laboratory access control with time-based permission management.
Gestion Énergétique
Surveillance and optimisation of water, electricity and air conditioning consumption in classrooms and dormitories.
Information Display
Campus announcements, class schedules and exam arrangements displayed on digital screens.
One-Card System
One card for dining, shopping, borrowing and access, convenient for campus life.

Solution Value

Improve campus safety management, optimise resource utilisation and enhance the campus experience for teachers and students. Supports integration with education management platforms.

Warehouse Management System

Inbound · Outbound · Inventory · Allocation

The Warehouse Management System (WMS) enables digital management of warehouse operations, improving inventory accuracy and operational efficiency.

Fonctionnalités clés

Inbound Management

Receiving, quality inspection, shelving and batch tracking of inbound goods.

Outbound Management

Picking, packing, shipping and carrier matching for outbound orders.

Inventory Management

Real-time inventory queries, cycle counting, safety stock alerts and expiry date management.

Allocation Management

Inter-warehouse transfers, replenishment suggestions and optimised picking paths.

Barcode / RFID

Full-process barcode or RFID tracking for accurate and efficient operations.

Report Analysis

Multi-dimensional reports including inventory turnover, inbound/outbound analysis and operator performance.

Avantages du système

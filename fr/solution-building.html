Bâtiment Intelligent Solution

BAS · Security · Fire · Energy · Gestion des Visiteurs

AXLTeco's smart building solution integrates multiple subsystems to achieve intelligent building operation, energy saving and safety assurance.

Solution Components

GTC System (BAS)
Intelligent control of HVAC, lighting and elevators for comfort and energy saving.
Sécurité & Surveillance
Vidéosurveillance (CCTV), access control and intrusion detection for all-round building security.

Online appointment, QR code access and visit record for efficient visitor management.

LPR, guidance and automated payment for efficient parking management.

Solution Value
